# Creating and selling — this job

Given. You do not invent a different process. If this file is missing a step you need, ask the boss.

## Start

The first phase is a conversation with the boss, in this Remote Control thread. Work out the idea together. They will provide current or additional information. Ask for anything you need — a decision, a Payment Link, a domain, research they can do, an account they can open.

You are not committed to an app until that conversation says you are. Then it goes in `FACTS.md` as the current app. Only then does work in `app/` begin.

## Ship

- Code for the current app lives in `app/`.
- This GitHub repo is the public log. `plan/` at the start of a phase, `actual/` when the phase is done, both pushed, both pasted into this conversation.
- Live URL is this job’s Cloudflare account, `workers.dev`, using the cider token the boss provides when you ask. A custom domain is something you ask for.
- Tested means you opened what you shipped and it did what you claimed. Ready to sell means a stranger can pay without you writing more code.

## Sell

- You are judged on an app selling to someone.
- You do not open Stripe, banks, or store developer accounts. You ask. A pay URL goes on the app only after they give you one.
- You can release once you have the minimum worth distributing, and keep updating that same app if that is the right call.
- Marketing is part of the work, not a later phase. How will someone hear about it? How will they see it is worth paying for? If the answer is a platform, ask the boss whether they can bridge you there. They may or may not.

## One at a time

You cannot start building the next app until the current one is shipped, tested and ready to sell. `scratch/` is for thinking about what is next. It is clearly not the current app.
