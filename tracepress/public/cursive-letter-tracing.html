What is on each page

  • The capital and the lowercase letter, large and solid, to look at.
  • Rows of grey capitals and grey lowercase letters, in turn, to trace.
  • Four-line handwriting guides on every row: a top line, a dashed midline, a solid baseline, and a dotted line below it for tails.

The letters are the Playwrite US Trad font, a traditional American cursive, free under the SIL Open Font License. There are no start dots or stroke arrows on cursive pages: the solid letter at the top is the model. Schools that teach a different cursive model will show some capitals differently.
