Free shape tracing worksheets

Six printable pages, one shape each: square, triangle, rectangle, diamond, star and heart. Each page shows the shape large, with a green dot numbered for where the pencil starts and a red arrow on every side for the way it goes. Below it are rows of the same shape in dots to trace.

Download the worksheets (PDF, 6 pages)

8.5" × 11", about 58 KB. The lines are 1" from top to bottom, the Trace Press setting for ages 4–5. Every shape reaches from the bottom line to the top line, the height of a capital letter. No sign-up. A small footer line on each page says where it came from.

How each shape is drawn

PageShapeStrokesHow it goes
1square1, 4 arrowsfrom the top left corner: down, across the bottom, up, and back across the top
2triangle1, 3 arrowsfrom the top point: down to the left, across the bottom, and back up to the top
3rectangle1, 4 arrowslike the square, from the top left corner, but 1.6 times as wide
4diamond1, 4 arrowsfrom the top point: down to the left, down to the bottom, up to the right, and back to the top
5star1, 10 arrowsfrom the top point, ten straight lines round to the left, in and out, back to the top
6heart2, 4 arrowstwo halves, numbered 1 and 2, both from the dip at the top: over the left bump and down to the point, then over the right bump and down to the point

Every shape but the heart is one line without lifting the pencil, and every one but the heart is made of straight lines only, the same down, across and slanted strokes as the tracing lines worksheets. Five of the six start on the top line, as 21 of the 26 capital letters do in Trace Press; the other five (C, G, O, Q and S) start just under it, on a curve. The heart starts at its dip, halfway between the dotted middle line and the top line.

Shape pages in a tracing book for KDP

The same six pages can open an A–Z letter tracing workbook you publish on Amazon KDP. Open Trace Press with Shapes ticked and the book is the six shape pages, then A to Z: 32 pages, with a full-wrap cover sized to match. Tick Pre-writing lines too and the four line pages go first, then the shapes, then A. You can add the numbers 0 to 9 after Z, and practice words after that.

For black ink on Amazon.com, KDP charges one flat printing price for any paperback of 24 to 110 pages, so the shape pages add nothing to the printing cost: $2.84 a copy at 8.5" × 11". The handwriting workbook guide has the full price table.
