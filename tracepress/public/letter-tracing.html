Free letter tracing worksheets with arrows, A to Z

26 printable pages, one for each letter of the alphabet, capital and lowercase together. At the top of each page is the letter pair, large. A green dot numbered for each stroke shows where the pencil starts, and a red arrow shows which way it goes.

Download the worksheets (PDF, A to Z)

8.5" × 11", about 220 KB, no sign-up. The lines are ¾" from top to bottom, the Trace Press setting for ages 5–7. A small footer line on each page says where it came from.

What is on each page

  • The capital and lowercase letter, large, with start dots and stroke arrows.
  • Two rows of dotted capitals and two rows of dotted lowercase letters to trace, in turn.
  • A row for each with one dotted letter at the start and room to write it alone.

The two letters on each page

Each page puts the capital and the lowercase letter side by side. Seven lowercase letters are their capital at half the height, drawn with the same strokes in the same order and direction: c, o, s, v, w, x and z. The other 19 change shape between the two cases. For example, u gains a line down its right side, and the lowercase b, d, p and q are each one stroke where the capital takes two.

Each green number is one stroke, and a page shows both letters' numbers. So a page takes from 2 to 5 strokes:

  • Two strokes a page (9): Cc, Gg, Ll, Oo, Ss, Uu, Vv, Ww, Zz.
  • Three strokes a page (7): Bb, Dd, Mm, Nn, Pp, Qq, Rr.
  • Four strokes a page (7): Aa, Hh, Jj, Kk, Tt, Xx, Yy.
  • Five strokes a page (3): Ee, Ff, Ii.

For one case alone, there are uppercase letter tracing worksheets (A to Z) and lowercase letter tracing worksheets (a to z), one letter to a page.

For joined-up writing, the cursive letter tracing worksheets are the same 26 pages in cursive.

The letters are vertical print, drawn in the stroke order and direction of a published school handwriting model. Schools that teach a slanted style will show some letters differently.

A different line size or page size
