Free lowercase letter tracing worksheets with arrows, a to z

26 printable pages of lowercase letters, one letter to a page. At the top of each page is the letter, large. A green dot numbered for each stroke shows where the pencil starts, and a red arrow shows which way it goes.

Download the worksheets (PDF, a to z)

8.5" × 11", about 130 KB, no sign-up. The lines are 1" from top to bottom, the Trace Press setting for ages 4–5. A small footer line on each page says where it came from.

What is on each page

  • The lowercase letter, large, with start dots and stroke arrows.
  • Two rows of dotted letters to trace.
  • Two rows with one dotted letter at the start and room to write it alone.

Want the capitals alone? The uppercase letter tracing worksheets are the same 26 pages for A to Z, and the letter tracing worksheets put both cases on each page.

The letters are vertical print, drawn in the stroke order and direction of a published school handwriting model. Schools that teach a slanted style will show some letters differently.

A different line size or page size

Trace Press makes the same 26 pages at any of four line sizes, from 1" lines for ages 4–5 down to smaller lines for older children, and at six page sizes. Choose "Lowercase only" under Letters, pick the sizes and download. It's free, and the free book is the whole book, with the same footer line. You can also add the numbers 0 to 9 and your own practice words.

Publishing it as a book on KDP

The pages are laid out to Amazon KDP's paperback rules, and Trace Press makes a full-wrap cover sized to the book.
