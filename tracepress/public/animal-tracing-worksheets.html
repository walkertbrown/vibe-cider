Animal tracing worksheets

Twenty animal words, one a page, to print and trace. Each page has a picture of the animal to colour in, the word large with numbered start dots and arrows on every letter, then rows of it in dots to trace on four-line guides, then lines to write it alone.

Download the worksheets (PDF, 20 worksheets)

8.5" × 11", no sign-up. The words, in order: cat, dog, pig, bat, rat, bug, fish, bird, deer, worm, horse, snail, panda, rabbit, turtle, spider, shrimp, dragon, squirrel and butterfly. They start with three-letter animals a child can sound out, cat, dog and pig, and build up to squirrel and butterfly. One of them is a dragon, because some children will ask.

Which letters the words practise

Between them, the twenty words use 21 of the 26 lowercase letters. The five they leave out are j, k, v, x and z. To practise every letter, add jacket, key, van, box and zeppelin to the words in the book maker: each has a picture, and each brings in one of the missing letters.

Tall letters and tails

On the four-line guides, seven lowercase letters rise to the headline: b, d, f, h, k, l and t. Five drop below the baseline: g, j, p, q and y. The other fourteen sit between the baseline and the dashed midline, apart from the dot on an i. One of these words stays between the baseline and the midline all the way: worm. Every letter in it is the same height. Eighteen of these words reach up to the headline: cat (t), dog (d), bat (b and t), rat (t), bug (b), fish (f and h), bird (b and d), deer (d), horse (h), snail (l), panda (d), rabbit (b and t), turtle (t and l), spider (d), shrimp (h), dragon (d), squirrel (l) and butterfly (b, t, f and l). Nine of these words drop below the baseline: dog (g), pig (p and g), bug (g), panda (p), spider (p), shrimp (p), dragon (g), squirrel (q) and butterfly (y).

The words by length and strokes

A stroke is one movement of the pencil, from putting it down to lifting it, and each letter on the page shows its strokes with numbered start dots. An i or j takes two: the line, then the dot. These words run from 3 to 13 strokes, 115 in all.

Ordered by strokes instead, fewest first, the words go dog, bug, cat, pig, bat, rat, deer, worm, bird, horse, panda, fish, snail, dragon, spider, shrimp, rabbit, turtle, squirrel and butterfly. Words with the same count keep their order above. The biggest change is fish: it has four letters but takes 6 strokes, as f and i take two each, so it moves from seventh to twelfth. To make the book in that order, open Trace Press with the words by strokes.

Selling an animal tracing book on KDP

A word gets a picture when Trace Press has one for it: 163 everyday words so far, such as dog, train and umbrella. A word without one gets the same page, without the picture. Eleven of the twenty pictures on this page are from Tabler Icons, under the MIT licence, and nine are from Lucide, under the ISC licence: rat, bird, worm, snail, panda, rabbit, turtle, shrimp and squirrel.
