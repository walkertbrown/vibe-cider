What the pages look like

Built to KDP's manuscript rules

  • Six KDP paperback trims, from 5" × 8" to 8.5" × 11", with or without bleed.
  • Inside, outside, top and bottom margins at KDP's minimums for the page count. Every mark sits 1/32" inside them, and the test suite renders every page to pixels to check that.
  • Every line at least 0.75 pt thick, every grey well darker than KDP's 10% minimum, and all text 7 pt or larger.
  • Fonts embedded (Liberation Sans, and Playwrite US Trad for cursive, under the SIL Open Font License). The print letters are drawn as pencil strokes, not set in a dotted font, so there is no tracing font to license.
  • Print or cursive. Cursive letters are joined the way the font joins them, every pair from aa to zz, and drawn as outlines, so what you see in the preview is what prints.
  • A full-wrap cover, back, spine and front on one page, sized for the book's page count and your paper, with the barcode area left clear (cover sizes for every trim and page count). Your title, subtitle and name, and a big traced "Aa" on the front.

Stroke order you can see

Every letter is drawn as the strokes a pencil makes, not as a font outline. The model letter numbers where each stroke starts, and red arrows show which way it goes. The letters follow a vertical manuscript (ball-and-stick) style.

Pre-writing lines, before A

Tick Pre-writing lines and the book opens with four pages of the strokes letters are built from: down and across lines, slants, zigzags and waves, circles and crosses. Each has a numbered start dot and arrows, like the letters. To print just those, the free tracing lines worksheets are the four pages as a PDF.

Shapes, before A

Tick Shapes and the book gets six pages before A, after the line pages if those are on: a square, triangle, rectangle, diamond, star and heart, one a page, each from the bottom line to the top line with a numbered start dot and an arrow on every side. To print just those, the free shape tracing worksheets are the six pages as a PDF.

Numbers 0–9

Tick Numbers 0–9 and the book gets ten more pages after Z, one for each digit, laid out like the letter pages: the number large with a numbered start dot and arrows, then rows to trace. Each follows a published school number formation chart: the 4 open with the short stroke first, the 5's hat last, the 8 as an S with a line back up. To print just the numbers, the free number tracing worksheets are the ten pages as a PDF.

Your own words, after Z

Type up to 52 words in the Practice words box. Each gets its own page: the word large with start dots and arrows on every letter, rows of it to trace, and blank lines to write it alone. The page count and the cover's spine width follow. 52 words keeps the book under 79 pages, the point where KDP starts allowing spine text, so the blank spine stays correct.
