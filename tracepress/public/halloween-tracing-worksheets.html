Free Halloween tracing worksheets

Twenty Halloween words, one a page, to print and trace. Each page has a picture of the word to colour in, the word large with numbered start dots and arrows on every letter, then rows of it in dots to trace on four-line guides, then lines to write it alone.

Every word is a thing a child can see in its picture. They run from three letters to eight, so the early pages suit a child just starting and the later ones a child who is writing words already.

Which letters the words practise

Between them, the twenty words use 22 of the 26 lowercase letters. The four they leave out are j, q, x and z. To practise every letter, add jacket, quilt, box and zeppelin to the words in the book maker: each has a picture, and each brings in one of the missing letters.

Tall letters and tails

All seven letters that rise to the headline of the four-line guides turn up here, b, d, f, h, k, l and t, though f only in leaf. Three letters with tails, g, p and y, drop below the baseline; there is no j or q. Two of these words stay between the baseline and the midline all the way: moon and acorn. Every letter in them is the same height. Seventeen of these words reach up to the headline: bat (b and t), cat (t), mask (k), leaf (l and f), bone (b), star (t), wand (d), skull (k and l), candy (d), ghost (h and t), apple (l), spider (d), candle (d and l), cookie (k), pumpkin (k), lollipop (l) and mushroom (h). Seven of these words drop below the baseline: candy (y), ghost (g), grave (g), apple (p), spider (p), pumpkin (p) and lollipop (p).

The words by length and strokes

Each letter on the page shows its strokes with numbered start dots, one dot for each time the pencil goes down. These twenty words have 101 letters between them and run from 4 to 9 strokes, 115 in all. Bat is the quickest at 4; pumpkin and lollipop take 9 each. Four words have an i, which takes two strokes, the line and then the dot: spider, cookie, pumpkin and lollipop.

Ordered by strokes instead, fewest first, the words go bat, cat, moon, bone, wand, mask, leaf, star, grave, apple, acorn, skull, candy, ghost, candle, spider, cookie, mushroom, pumpkin and lollipop. Words with the same count keep their order above. One of the biggest changes is skull: it has five letters but takes 6 strokes, as k takes two, so it moves from ninth to twelfth.
