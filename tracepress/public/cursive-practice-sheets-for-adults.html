Free cursive practice sheets for adults

Cursive practice at a grown-up size: one page for each letter, A to Z, on 0.45" handwriting lines instead of the big lines made for small children. Each page shows the capital and the lowercase letter in solid cursive, then rows of them in grey to write over, then rows with one letter to start you off.

Download the sheets (PDF, 26 pages)

8.5" × 11", about 115 KB, no sign-up. Print the letters you want, or all of them. A small footer line says where they came from.

What is on each sheet

  • The letter as a model, capital and lowercase, in solid cursive.
  • Rows of the capital and of the lowercase letter in light grey, to trace.
  • Rows that start with one grey letter and leave the rest of the line to you.
  • Four-line guides on every row: a top line, a dashed midline, a solid baseline and a dotted line for tails. The 0.45" is the height from the top line to the baseline.

The letters are the Playwrite US Trad font, a traditional American cursive, free under the SIL Open Font License. Other cursive models shape some capitals differently.

Other sizes, and whole words

The same sheets come in bigger lines for children: the cursive letter tracing worksheets use 0.75" lines. To copy from rather than trace, the cursive alphabet chart has every letter on one page. To practise joining whole words, open Trace Press on cursive at 0.45", type the words into the word box, and preview the pages in your browser.

Making a cursive workbook for adults to sell?

Trace Press makes the whole book, the letters, the numbers and any practice words, with its cover, laid out to Amazon KDP's paperback rules, at any of four line sizes and six page sizes. The cursive handwriting workbook guide has the page counts, cover sizes and print cost.
