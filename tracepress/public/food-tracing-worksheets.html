Food tracing worksheets

Twenty food words, one a page, to print and trace. Each page has a picture of the food to colour in, the word large with numbered start dots and arrows on every letter, then rows of it in dots to trace on four-line guides, then lines to write it alone.

Download the worksheets (PDF, 20 pages)

8.5" × 11", no sign-up. The words, in order: egg, nut, cake, milk, soup, bean, pizza, bread, apple, lemon, grape, donut, carrot, cheese, cookie, banana, cherry, burger, cupcake and broccoli. Fruit, vegetables and a few treats, from three-letter words a child can sound out, egg and nut, up to cupcake and broccoli.

Your own words

To trace a child's name or a different list, type the words into the free tracing worksheet generator and print one page. For short first words with pictures, there are word tracing worksheets with pictures, for vehicles, transportation tracing worksheets, and for animals, animal tracing worksheets; for seasonal words, there are Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas tracing worksheets.

Selling a food tracing book on KDP

Trace Press makes the whole book: A to Z, then these words a page each, with a matching full-wrap cover, in any of six KDP trim sizes. The button below opens it with the twenty food words already typed in, for a 46-page book. Change the words, add numbers 0 to 9 or a “This book belongs to” page, and download. For the cover's size at that page count, see tracing book cover size.

A word gets a picture when Trace Press has one for it: 163 everyday words so far, such as dog, train and umbrella. A word without one gets the same page, without the picture. The outlines come from Tabler Icons, under the MIT licence, and, for some animals and foods Tabler doesn't draw, such as rabbit and broccoli, from Lucide, under the ISC licence. Both allow copies to be sold. The licence texts: Tabler Icons, Lucide.
