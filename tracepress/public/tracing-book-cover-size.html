Tracing book cover size for KDP

A KDP paperback cover is one PDF: back, spine and front side by side, with 0.125" of bleed on every outside edge. Its width depends on the trim, the page count and the paper, because the spine is as thick as the pages. These are the sizes for the tracing books Trace Press makes, from 26 to 78 pages.

A 26-page 8.5" × 11" cover as the free version makes it, PREVIEW mark included (PDF).

The arithmetic, from KDP's cover help page

  • Spine = page count × paper thickness: 0.002252" a page on white paper, 0.0025" on cream.
  • Cover width = 0.125" bleed + trim width (back) + spine + trim width (front) + 0.125" bleed.
  • Cover height = 0.125" + trim height + 0.125".

Which page count is your book?

  • 26: A to Z, one letter a page.
  • 30: with four pages of pre-writing lines before A.
  • 36: with the numbers 0 to 9 after Z.
  • 40: lines and numbers both, like the preschool tracing worksheets.
  • 78: the most it makes, with practice words filling the rest.

A “This book belongs to” page adds one. Anything in between follows the same arithmetic.

The spine stays blank

KDP allows text on the spine only from 79 pages. Every tracing book here is 78 pages or fewer, so its spine is left blank. KDP also asks for words at least 0.125" inside the trim and a clear 2" × 1.2" area for the barcode on the back; Trace Press keeps the front's words 0.375" in and leaves that box empty.
