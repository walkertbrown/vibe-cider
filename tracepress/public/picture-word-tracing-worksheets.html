Word tracing worksheets with pictures

Twenty first words, one a page, to print and trace. Each page has a picture of the word to colour in, the word large with numbered start dots and arrows on every letter, then rows of it in dots to trace on four-line guides, then lines to write it alone.

Download the worksheets (PDF, 20 pages)

8.5" × 11", no sign-up. The words, in order: cat, dog, sun, bus, cup, egg, pig, bed, car, key, fish, ball, cake, moon, tree, star, book, shoe, boat, apple. They are short, everyday things a young child can name from the picture, three to five letters long, so the picture helps them read the word they are writing.

Which letters the words practise

Between them, the twenty words use 20 of the 26 lowercase letters. The six they leave out are j, q, v, w, x and z. To practise every letter, add jacket, quilt, van, watch, box and zeppelin to the words in the book maker: each has a picture, and each brings in one of the missing letters.

Tall letters and tails

On the four-line guides, seven lowercase letters rise to the headline: b, d, f, h, k, l and t. Five drop below the baseline: g, j, p, q and y. The other fourteen sit between the baseline and the dashed midline, apart from the dot on an i. Three of these words stay between the baseline and the midline all the way: sun, car and moon. Every letter in them is the same height. Fourteen of these words reach up to the headline: cat (t), dog (d), bus (b), bed (b and d), key (k), fish (f and h), ball (b and l), cake (k), tree (t), star (t), book (b and k), shoe (h), boat (b and t) and apple (l). Six of these words drop below the baseline: dog (g), cup (p), egg (g), pig (p and g), key (y) and apple (p).

The words by length and strokes

A stroke is one movement of the pencil, from putting it down to lifting it, and each letter on the page shows its strokes with numbered start dots. An i or j takes two: the line, then the dot. These words run from 3 to 6 strokes, 82 in all.

Ordered by strokes instead, fewest first, the words go dog, sun, bus, cup, egg, bed, car, cat, pig, ball, moon, shoe, key, cake, tree, star, book, boat, apple and fish. Words with the same count keep their order above. The biggest change is fish: it has four letters but takes 6 strokes, as f and i take two each, so it moves from eleventh to twentieth. To make the book in that order, open Trace Press with the words by strokes.
