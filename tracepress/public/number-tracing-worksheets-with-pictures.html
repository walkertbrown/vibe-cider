Free number tracing worksheets with pictures, 0 to 9

Ten printable pages, one number to a page. Each page has the number large, with a numbered start dot and stroke arrows, then that many stars to count and colour, with the number word under them: one star on the 1 page, three on the 3 page, nine on the 9 page. Then rows of dotted numbers to trace.

Download the worksheets (PDF, 0 to 9)

8.5" × 11", about 85 KB, no sign-up. The lines are 1" from top to bottom, the Trace Press setting for ages 4–5. A small footer line on each page says where it came from.

Trace it, count it, read it

On each page the child traces the number, counts the stars, colours them in and reads the word: "seven" under seven stars, next to a 7. The stars are outlines with nothing filled in. Up to three sit in a row; four make a square; five to nine are rows of three, with a short last row centred, so seven is three, three and one.

The 0 page has no stars, since there is nothing to count. It has the 0 to trace, like the others.

What is on each page

  • The number, large, with a numbered start dot and stroke arrows showing which way to go.
  • Stars to count, as many as the number, and the number word under them in bold.
  • Two rows of dotted numbers to trace, then rows with one dotted number and room to write it alone.

The star is from Tabler Icons (MIT licence), free to use in a book you sell.

Publishing it as a book on KDP

The pages are laid out to Amazon KDP's paperback rules, and Trace Press makes a full-wrap cover sized to the book.
