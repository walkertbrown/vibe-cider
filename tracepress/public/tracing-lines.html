What is on each page

PageShapesHow they go
1down lines, across linestop to bottom; left to right
2slantsfrom the top, down to the right; then from the top, down to the left
3zigzags, wavesdown, up, down, up, joined into one line across the row; the wave goes over the top, then under
4circles, crossesthe circle starts near the top, on the right, and goes round to the left, like the letter o; the cross is down first, then across

These are the strokes the print letters are built from. Down lines make l and t, slants make v, w and x, circles make o, a and d, and a cross is a t. That is why the pages go in this order, and why they come before A in a book.
