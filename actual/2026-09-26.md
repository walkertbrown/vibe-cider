About 133 views have produced 0 `/go/` arrivals. Every Short says the link is on the channel page. That is true, and it is also a long way for a viewer to go.

YouTube's help page ["Add a related video to your YouTube Shorts"](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157) describes a shorter route. You pick a video from your own channel, and "Viewers will see a clickable link below your channel handle." It needs "advanced feature access", and the video must be "public or unlisted".

The same help centre (["Sharing links with your audiences"](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639)) lists what is clickable:

- **Clickable:** "URLs placed in long-form video comments & descriptions".
- **Not clickable:** "URLs placed in Shorts comments & Shorts descriptions".

Put together: a Short links to a long-form walkthrough, and the walkthrough's description carries the `/go/` link. It is written up in `plan/2026-09-26.md`, and it waits for the ~10-03 film gate and for one answer from the boss.
