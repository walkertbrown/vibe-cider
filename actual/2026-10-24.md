# Actual — 2026-10-24

## Every sale now says where it came from

Puzzle Press has had one sale. That buyer arrived from a Google search, but the sale itself didn't say so. The Buy button was the bare Stripe link, and the source was worked out afterwards by matching the analytics referrer to the minute of the payment.

Now both Buy buttons, on [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) and on [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/), carry a short source tag to Stripe, kept on the payment record. The tag is set once per browser, the first time you open any page of either site. If the link you followed had `?ref=<name>`, that name is the tag. Otherwise it is the site that linked to you, such as `google_com`. If you came from another page of the same site, it names that page, and if nothing linked to you it is `direct`. The tag is only ever a site or page name, never a search or anything you typed. Both privacy pages say what is kept in your browser and what goes to Stripe.

At first only the generator recorded the source, so someone who landed on a guide page from Google and then clicked into the tool was recorded as coming from the guide. By the afternoon, every page on both sites records it. A test reads every page and fails if one doesn't. The one exception is the comparison page, which is being left unchanged through Sunday.

## Three generators, each in about thirty seconds

Three new raw clips went to the house channel. Each opens on one of the measured tables now on the guide pages, then makes a 20-puzzle, 32-page book on a phone with no sign-up.

- [Maze book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/maze-book-generator): 2,000 mazes, every one with exactly one route. Grid size alone doesn't set difficulty: the longest easy route was 179 cells, longer than the shortest hard one at 105. The clip makes a graded book and shows its first and last mazes.
- [Crossword book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/crossword-book-generator): 7,200 crosswords, every answer with its clue. In these themed grids the middle puzzle crosses only about one letter in seven, so most letters come from the clue. That is why the clues are written in plain language.
- [Word search book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-search-book-generator): 5,400 puzzles, with every word placed in every grid. The filler letters are random, and the generator throws away any fill that spells one of 37 rude or offensive strings. Every one of the 90 easy and medium books needed at least one fill thrown away, as did 89 of the 90 hard books. A 20-puzzle book of random letters that nobody checked would be likely to carry one. Here a rejected fill is simply redone, so the PDF only ever gets grids that passed.

The Halloween tracing clip's description now leads with its own count instead of the product name: twenty words, 115 strokes, from bat at 4 to pumpkin and lollipop at 9. The [Halloween tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets) page has the full list.

There were no sales, and one or two people a day ran either generator.
