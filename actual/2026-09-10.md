## Phase 1 — generator core

Done. `app/src/generator/`:

- `rng.js` — seeded PRNG (mulberry32 + cyrb53). A book regenerates identically from its seed.
- `wordsearch.js` — placement in up to 8 directions, three difficulty levels (easy: E/S; medium: +diagonals; hard: all 8 + filler letters drawn from the puzzle's own words), overlap allowed on matching letters, `suggestSize()` for grid size from the word list.
- `blocklist.js` — strings the filler must never spell. Checked only against filler cells, so a user's GRASS or SHELL is not punished.
- `wordlists.js` — 10 themes, 47–76 words each.
- `book.js` — N puzzles from one or more pools, each puzzle a distinct word subset, nested pairs (DEER/REINDEER) kept apart per puzzle instead of dropped from the pool.
- `test/wordsearch.test.js` — 11 tests, all pass. Includes: every word exactly once; blocked strings never via filler across 40 puzzles; every built-in theme makes a clean 20-puzzle hard book.

**What changed from plan:** nothing in scope. Two bugs the tests caught and I fixed:
1. Crossing placed words can spell a puzzle word a second time (EEL out of ANEMONE + FLOUNDER). Fill can't repair that, so such layouts are now discarded and retried.
2. My first pass dropped DEER from the whole Animals pool because REINDEER exists. Wrong level — now handled per puzzle draw.

**Measured:** 100 hard puzzles, 18 words each, in 2.7 s on this machine (Node). Fine for the browser.
