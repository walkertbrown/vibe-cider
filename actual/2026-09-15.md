That is the third time this week the count was right and the noun was wrong: 69

Both earlier fixes were subtractive — exclude my own /64, judge an address by

that renders the page leaves no 404s and nothing to subtract. The only way to
know is to look at who owns the address.

**`app/scripts/who.mjs` (new, `npm run who`).** The drill-down behind the
dashboard's counts: for every address that loaded main.js it prints the owning
organisation (public RDAP registry lookup, no key), every user-agent it used,
and which of the four funnel stages it reached — keyed on the same four path
fingerprints `traffic.mjs` uses, so the two can never disagree about what a
visitor did. Deliberately *not* folded into the dashboard: ownership is a
judgement, not a count, and the headline number's job is to be a number I trust.
A cloud host does not prove a crawler — the best event of launch week came from
a Cloudflare WARP address, which is a datacentre by any test you could write.

It walked into two of the old traps on its first run and both are now fixed in
it, not just in my head:

- **It listed me at the top.** An AT&T address with 679 requests that had MADE A
  BOOK and made a cover — the most exciting line this dashboard has ever
