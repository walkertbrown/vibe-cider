# 2026-09-15 — launch day

## Phase 29 — marketing/ leaves the public repo (00:2x–00:3x CT, before launch)

Boss approved the move I had proposed three times. Done: `git mv app/marketing
marketing`, so it now lives at the top of the private repo only and is no longer
inside the subtree that mirrors to `github.com/walkertbrown/puzzle-press`.

Eight files left the public tree: `launch-day.md` (traffic baselines, rollback
ids, thresholds), `answers.md` (prepared replies for launch threads),
`pins.md` (the Pinterest spam block and the workaround), `support.md`,
`ph-schedule-packet.md`, `product-hunt.md`, `show-hn.md`, `ai-crawlers.md`.
None of it was secret. The reason is audience: on Wednesday Show HN points
strangers at that repo, and "here are my scripted answers to your questions" is
a bad first impression of an honest thing.

**Two tests read that directory**, and breaking a public clone's suite on Show
HN day would have been the exact embarrassment I was trying to avoid.
`test/copy.test.js` walks `marketing/` for every .md to check numbers against the
code, and `test/launch-assets.mjs` reads `product-hunt.md` to check the launch
copy against the shipped images. Both now look in `./marketing` then
`../marketing`, and when it is in neither — which is what a public clone is —
they check everything else and say so. The guards exist to stop *me* publishing a
wrong number, and I am the one with the directory.

**Verified rather than assumed.** Split the subtree into a detached worktree,
which is exactly the tree that gets pushed, and ran both tests inside it:
copy.test.js 8/8 green, launch-assets green with "marketing/product-hunt.md not
in this checkout — images checked, launch copy not". Full suite 92/92 here.
Also updated the four source comments that pointed at `marketing/…` so they say
where it went, and replaced the README's layout row with a paragraph explaining
the absence — an unexplained gap invites the question the move was meant to
avoid.

**No deploy.** Nothing under `public/` changed; the site is untouched and still
`eaeedff1`, rollback `b8068dc0`.

**What this does not do:** the files are still in the public repo's *history*.
Removing them from HEAD is what stops a browser seeing them; erasing them
entirely means rewriting history and force-pushing a public repo, which I have
not done and would want asked for explicitly.
