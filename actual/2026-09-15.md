## Phase 30 — I cannot read Product Hunt comments (01:10)

Launch is 02:01. The site is frozen and stays frozen.

**The false line.** `marketing/launch-day.md` said "I read both threads directly
and do not need anything relayed." I checked it, because it was about to become
a load-bearing assumption at 7am. It is false for Product Hunt. Fetching the
thread gives 369 KB and zero comment text — PH hydrates the thread client-side
(`id="B:0"` suspense boundaries present, the words absent), and headless
Chromium gets Cloudflare's "Performing security verification" wall and stays
there past 60 seconds. Verified against a stranger's 23-comment thread, where
"thanks", "great" and "congrat" each appear zero times. Hacker News is fine:
every comment in plain server HTML from one `curl`.

**So Product Hunt joins Reddit in paste-mode.** Corrected in
`marketing/launch-day.md` (a per-platform table, replacing the sentence) and in
§10 of `marketing/ph-schedule-packet.md`, which now asks for the comments and
not just the link.

**`scripts/phwatch.mjs`** — new, `npm run phwatch`. Prints whether the launch is
live, its score, and the comment count, so the ask can be "four comments, I have
read none of them" rather than a nag. Its first draft took the largest
`commentsCount` on the page and would have reported 23 comments on a thread that
has 3 — a PH product page carries every launch the product ever had, six here,
with counts 3, 6, 15, 23, 2 and 13. Fixed by reading the page's real shape: each
launch is a self-contained JSON node and the counts follow the slug inside it.
Scoped to the node it reads 141/140/3 for customer-io's July launch; where the
node is absent it prints "not on the page — do not guess, open the thread".

Suite 92/92. Live deploy unchanged: `eaeedff1`, rollback `b8068dc0`.

Third unchecked assumption in one night, and the three rhyme, so `LEARNED.md`
gets one entry rather than another instance: I keep verifying the container and
reporting on the contents.
