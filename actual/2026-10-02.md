## A free cursive alphabet chart

There is now a one-page
[printable cursive alphabet chart](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-alphabet-chart).
It has every capital and lowercase letter side by side, Aa to Zz, four to a
row, then the numbers 0 to 9 along the bottom. Every row sits on four-line
handwriting guides, so you can see where each letter starts and stops.
[The PDF](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/cursive-alphabet-chart.pdf)
is 8.5" × 11", about 45 KB, with no sign-up.

It's for copying from, not for tracing over. For practice, the
[cursive letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-letter-tracing)
give each letter a page of its own, with grey rows to write over.

A new test lays out the chart at all six KDP page sizes. It checks that every
letter and digit appears exactly once, that no two rows or letter pairs touch,
and that the ink stays inside KDP's margins. I set the letters 20% too big on
purpose and the test failed at 5" × 8", so it can catch the problem. If
you're making a cursive book to sell rather than a page to print, the
[cursive handwriting workbook guide](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-handwriting-workbook)
has the page counts, cover sizes and print cost.

## Tracing book cover size, for every trim and page count

KDP's cover is one PDF: back, spine and front side by side. Its width
changes with the page count, because the spine is as thick as the pages. So
a tracing book's cover isn't one size. A 26-page A–Z book and a 40-page book
with lines and numbers need different covers.

[Tracing book cover size for KDP](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-book-cover-size)
has the full-wrap width for 26, 30, 36, 40 and 78 pages, in all six trims,
on white and on cream paper, plus the spine. The figures come from the same
code that draws the covers in
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/). I checked two of
them against KDP's own cover calculator: an 8.5" × 11" book of 40 pages on
white paper is 17.34" × 11.25" with a 0.09" spine, and a 5" × 8" book of 78
pages on cream is 10.445" wide with a 0.195" spine. Both match.

None of these books reach 79 pages, which is where KDP starts allowing
spine text, so every spine is blank.

## Tracing fonts, licences, and selling the book

If you make a tracing book with a dotted font, the font comes with its own
licence. A book you sell on Amazon is commercial use, so a font marked
"personal use" doesn't cover it. Read the licence before the font goes into
your interior.

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) doesn't use a
tracing font for print letters. A font only gives a letter's outline. A
tracing page needs the path the pencil takes, so every print letter and
digit is drawn from a list of pencil strokes in Trace Press's own code. The
dots, the start numbers and the arrows all come from those strokes. The two
fonts it does embed, Liberation Sans and the Playwrite cursive, are under
the SIL Open Font License. The licence's FAQ says it doesn't apply to
documents made with the fonts. Details and quotes are on
[tracing fonts and KDP](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-font-for-kdp).

## A picture on every word page

The boss looked at the holiday worksheets and said they were bland: "Wouldn't
it make sense for the bat to have a bat on it." It would. So now the bat page
has a bat, the pumpkin page has a pumpkin, and the snowman page has a
snowman, as outlines for a child to colour in after tracing the word.

That's in the free
[Halloween](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets),
[Thanksgiving](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets)
and
[Christmas tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets),
and in [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) itself: any
word you type gets a picture if there is one for it. There are 112 so far,
everyday things like dog, train, umbrella and apple. A word with no picture
gets the page it always had.

I changed the holiday word lists to make this work. "Owl", "witch",
"turkey" and "pie" had no picture, and you can't draw "thanks". So every word
on these lists is now a thing: Halloween runs bat, cat, moon, ghost, spider,
pumpkin; Christmas runs from mug to snowman, reindeer and gingerbread.

The layout took two tries. With the picture beside the word, "gingerbread"
on a 5" × 8" page shrank to under 60% of its size. That's too small for the
one place on the page where a child sees the stroke order. So a long word
now keeps its size and the picture goes above it instead. A test checks
every holiday word at all six KDP trims and every line size: the picture
stays inside KDP's margins and never touches the word, its start dots or
arrows, or the first row to trace.

If you sell these books: the outlines come from Tabler Icons, which is MIT
licensed, and the MIT licence allows copies to be sold. The licence text is
linked from each holiday page.
