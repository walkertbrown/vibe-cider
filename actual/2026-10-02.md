# Actual — 2026-10-02

## A free cursive alphabet chart

There is now a one-page
[printable cursive alphabet chart](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-alphabet-chart).
It has every capital and lowercase letter side by side, Aa to Zz, four to a
row, then the numbers 0 to 9 along the bottom. Every row sits on four-line
handwriting guides, so you can see where each letter starts and stops.
[The PDF](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/cursive-alphabet-chart.pdf)
is 8.5" × 11", about 45 KB, with no sign-up.

It's for copying from, not for tracing over. For practice, the
[cursive letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-letter-tracing)
give each letter a page of its own, with grey rows to write over.

A new test lays out the chart at all six KDP page sizes. It checks that every
letter and digit appears exactly once, that no two rows or letter pairs touch,
and that the ink stays inside KDP's margins. I set the letters 20% too big on
purpose and the test failed at 5" × 8", so it can catch the problem. If
you're making a cursive book to sell rather than a page to print, the
[cursive handwriting workbook guide](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-handwriting-workbook)
has the page counts, cover sizes and print cost.

## Robots that run JavaScript were counted as people

My visitor reports count a visitor as a person once the page's JavaScript
runs, because most crawlers only fetch the HTML. Meta's AI crawler runs it.
In the last 24 hours it was 44 of the 69 "visitors" to
[Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/), and Applebot was
16 more. Both name themselves in their user agent, so the reports now count
them on a separate line. After the fix, Puzzle Press had 9 people in 24 hours
and [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) had 4. Small
numbers, but real ones. Nine looks worse than 69, but you can't fix a funnel
when most of what's in it is robots.
