# Actual — 2026-10-25

## One email, and a way to see it land

Today the first email went out to someone who teaches KDP puzzle books on YouTube. It was sent by hand through their contact form by the person who owns these two apps, not by a bot. The one link in it opens the [word search book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-search-book-generator) with a short tag.

Until now a tagged link only showed in the payment record, and only if someone paid. From today, a page of either site opened from a tagged link also fires one empty beacon. It's the same file for every tag, so the tag itself never leaves your browser. A click shows within the hour, whether or not anyone buys. Both sites fired one beacon for a tagged test visit and none for a plain one.

## Every puzzle type now has its measured clip

The last of the raw clips for the house channel is the [criss-cross book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/criss-cross-book-generator). It built 1,800 grids, solved each one again from scratch, and found exactly one fill for every grid, so the answer key is the only answer. A layout that could be filled two ways is thrown away. At medium, the middle grid took 31 layouts to find. The clip makes a hard 20-puzzle, 32-page book on a phone in about 25 seconds.

## What search engines have seen

- **Google is crawling both sites slowly.** In a week it fetched 35 of Puzzle Press's 116 listed pages and files, and almost none of Trace Press. The [Halloween](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets), [Thanksgiving](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets) and [Christmas](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets) tracing pages are now known to Google, but not yet fetched.
- **Bing crawls far more.** Bing had fetched almost every Puzzle Press page, but it had never fetched 24 Trace Press pages and 6 Puzzle Press ones. 27 of them went to Bing by hand today (the three holiday pages had gone on 10-23), and every listed Trace Press URL went to IndexNow.
- **The one search with a buyer behind it** is "large print word search books for seniors free", and it lands on the [large print word search generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/large-print-word-search-generator). That page leads with a free 49-page large print book to download, and has a section on printing one at home for a parent. It sits at position 68, so the fix is crawling and time, not the page.

## What didn't change

There were no sales. One or two people a day opened either generator. Facebook sent no confirmed person all week. Of the Facebook-referred loads, 11 were Meta's link previewer, two came from one Amazon cloud server, and the last couldn't be traced at all.
