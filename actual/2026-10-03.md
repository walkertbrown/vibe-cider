# Actual — 2026-10-03

## "A is for apple": a picture on every letter page

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) has a new book option:
**A picture on each letter page**. Tick it and each letter page gets a simple
outline to colour of a word that starts with that letter: apple on A, ball on B,
cat on C, all the way to zeppelin on Z. The word is printed under the picture.
X gets "box", the way many alphabet books handle it.

Q gets nothing for now. The only Q picture in either icon set I use is a chess
queen, and a four-year-old won't see a chess piece as a queen.

Your letter page keeps everything else: the large model letter with numbered
start dots and arrows, the dotted rows to trace, and the rows to write the
letter alone.

**Where the picture goes:**
- On a wide page, it sits to the right of the model letters.
- On a small trim with big lines, where it would be cramped there, it moves
  above the letters and the rows shift down.

The word under it is never smaller than 7pt, KDP's minimum for type.

A test lays out every pictured letter in all six KDP trim sizes, four line sizes,
and capitals, lowercase or both: 1,800 pages. On every page it checks four
things:
- the ink stays inside KDP's margins;
- the picture keeps a clear gap from the letters;
- the word clears the first tracing row;
- the word is at least 7pt.

The pictures are outlines from Tabler Icons and Lucide, both free for
commercial use, so you can sell a book that has them in it.
[Animal](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/animal-tracing-worksheets),
[food](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/food-tracing-worksheets) and
[transportation](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/transportation-tracing-worksheets)
word pages already use them. There are 162 pictures now.

If you want letter pages without a picture to print right away, the
[free letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing)
are still there.

The words run from apple to zeppelin; the Q page is plain. If you want another
line size or trim, or numbers and practice words added, a link on the same

Making the sample showed up a flaw: when a picture sat beside the big model
letters, that row's handwriting lines ran right through it, across the rabbit,
the apple and every picture placed beside the letters. A child colouring it in would be colouring
over lines. The lines now stop just before the picture, on letter pages and on
word pages, in print and in cursive. The free
[animal](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/animal-tracing-worksheets),
[food](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/food-tracing-worksheets) and
holiday word PDFs have been remade with the fix. The tests now check every
picture page for it, and they fail on the old layout.

## Google has indexed Trace Press, and our sitemaps were stale

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) is in Google's index
as of this morning: the home page, crawled 10-03 at
02:28 UTC. The other 27 pages aren't indexed yet, and Google has never heard
of 23 of them. So I looked at why.

Google finds a new page two ways: a link from a page it already knows, and
the sitemap. Both were broken in the same quiet way.

- **The links.** [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/)
  links to Trace Press from its home page and from the
  [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator),
  which is the page Google shows most often. But Google last crawled those
  pages on September 22, before Trace Press existed.
- **The sitemap.** A sitemap's `lastmod` date is how a site tells Google a page
  is worth fetching again. Ours still said September 29 for pages that changed
  on October 2. I had a check for exactly this, but it ran only when I
  remembered to run it.

Now the check runs with every test, and a deploy can't go out with a stale
date. Trace Press got the same check, which found 38 of its 54 dates were
wrong. Every date is the day the file was last really changed, never today's
date stamped on everything, which search engines learn to ignore.

If you publish your own site for your books, it's worth a look: open your
`sitemap.xml` and compare a page you changed last week with its `lastmod`.

## Q is for quilt

The "A is for apple" letter pages had one hole: Q. Neither free icon set I use
has a Q picture a child would know. There's a chess queen, a QR code, a
question mark and quotation marks. So the Q page was a plain letter page in an
otherwise pictured A–Z book.

Now Q is for quilt: a patchwork block drawn for Trace Press in the same style
as the other outlines. It's a rounded square of nine patches, with a triangle
in each corner and a diamond in the middle, and every shape is closed so a
child can colour it in. It sits beside the letters like the apple and the
rabbit, with "quilt" under it.

You can use it in a book you sell, like the other pictures. The free
[alphabet tracing worksheets with pictures](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/alphabet-tracing-worksheets-with-pictures)
now run apple to zeppelin with no gap. The
[workbook maker](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) puts the same
pages in any of the six KDP trim sizes.

## Stars to count on the number pages

With "A picture on each letter page" ticked, a
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) book had a picture on
every letter from apple to zeppelin, then ten plain number pages after Z. Now
the [number pages](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/number-tracing)
join in: page 1 has one star, page 3 has three, page 9 has nine, each an outline
to colour, with the number word under them. So the child traces the 3, counts
three stars and reads "three" on the same page. The 0 page stays plain, since
there's nothing to count.

The stars sit where the letter pictures sit: beside the big model number on a
wide page, above it on a small trim with big lines. A short row is centred, so
seven stars are a row of three, a row of three and one in the middle.

The option is now called **A picture on each letter and number page**. Tick it
and add the numbers 0–9, and the counting pages come with them.

A test lays out every number in all six KDP trim sizes and four line sizes. On
each page it checks:
- the right number of stars;
- that no two stars touch;
- that everything stays inside KDP's margins and clear of the big number;
- that the word is at least 7pt and clear of the first tracing row.

The test fails if a star goes missing or the stars grow into each other.

your tracing book has pictures, the cover should say so.

Now it does. Tick "A picture on each letter and number page" and the cover

## What Google has seen so far, and the little icon beside a result

Trace Press went live on September 29. I checked every one of its 30 pages with
Google's URL inspection. The home page is indexed. One page has been crawled
and not yet indexed. Four are queued, and the other 24 Google hasn't reached
yet, although it has read the sitemap that lists all of them. In two days
Googlebot fetched two pages. A new site gets crawled slowly, so the free
pages, like the
[alphabet tracing worksheets with pictures](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/alphabet-tracing-worksheets-with-pictures),
mostly don't show up in search yet.

One thing in the crawl log was fixable today. Google asked for the site's
icon, the small square it shows beside a search result, and there wasn't one
it could fetch. The pages drew the icon from text inside the page itself,
which a browser tab can show but Google's crawler has no file to download.
Google's guidance is a square file at least 8px across, ideally over 48px,
at an address its crawler can reach.

Both apps now have one: a green T for Trace Press and a navy P for
[Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator),
at 48px and as a scalable SVG, on every page. A test fails if any page goes
back to an icon Google can't fetch.

If you run a site for your own books: open yoursite.com/favicon.ico in a
browser. If you get a "not found" page, Google sees the same thing.

## Two letter pages that finally say different things

The free [uppercase letter tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/uppercase-letter-tracing)
and [lowercase letter tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/lowercase-letter-tracing)
pages were near twins: 87% of each page's wording was the same as the other's.
To a search engine two pages like that look like one page twice. So each one
now covers the thing that is different about its letters.

**Capitals: how many strokes each one takes.**
- One stroke (9): C, G, L, O, S, U, V, W, Z
- Two strokes (12): B, D, J, K, M, N, P, Q, R, T, X, Y
- Three strokes (4): A, F, H, I
- Four strokes (1): E

The capitals all fill the space from the baseline to the top line. Only Q's
tail goes below the baseline.

**Lowercase: three heights.**
- Small letters, up to the dotted midline (13): a, c, e, m, n, o, r, s, u, v, w, x, z
- Tall letters, up to the top line (7): b, d, f, h, k, l, t
- Letters that hang below the baseline (5): g, j, p, q, y

Seven lowercase letters take two strokes: f, i, j, k, t, x, y. The rest take
one.

These aren't copied from a chart. They are read straight from the letter
shapes Trace Press draws in your book, and a test checks the pages against
those shapes. If a letter's strokes ever change, the test fails until the page
says so too.

If you're planning a KDP tracing book, the lists also give you a way to
order practice words: "sun" and "cow" are all one-stroke letters, so a child
can trace each letter without lifting the pencil. Trace Press itself runs its
letter pages A to Z.

## Which letters a holiday tracing book leaves out

If you sell a Christmas tracing book, the words in it decide which letters a
child practises. None of the three holiday lists here covers all 26. So each free holiday
page now says which letters its words cover, which they skip, and how to fill
the gap.

- The [Christmas tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets)
  use 21 of the 26 lowercase letters. They leave out j, q, v, x and z.
- The [Halloween tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets)
  use 22 and leave out j, q, x and z.
- The [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets)
  use 21 and leave out j, q, v, x and z.

To make a book that practises every letter, add the picture words from the
A–Z pages: jacket, quilt, van, box and zeppelin. Each has an outline picture,
so the page looks like the rest of the book. Twenty-five words fit easily: the
book takes up to 52 pages after Z.

Each page also has a table of its twenty words with the letters and pencil
strokes in each. A stroke is one movement from putting the pencil down to
lifting it, and the page shows each one as a numbered start dot. "mug" is 3
strokes; "gingerbread" is 12. If you want an easy first half and a harder
second half, the table gives you the order.

The counts come from the letter shapes Trace Press draws, and a test checks
each page's table against them and against the words its "Make a book" button
opens. If a word or a letter changes, the test fails until the page is updated.
