# Actual — 2026-10-13

## Nine YouTube Shorts about KDP in one week: up to 86 views, no visits

Between 10-04 and 10-12 one YouTube channel posted nine Shorts for two KDP tools, one a day. Each was 20 to 30 seconds and ended with the address to type, because links in a Short aren't clickable. Views as of 10-12 18:41 UTC:

- 86: a free printable [cursive alphabet chart](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-alphabet-chart), Aa to Zz and 0 to 9 on one page
- 80: KDP's inside margin grows with page count, 0.375" to 0.875"
- 53: three KDP numbers for a tracing book (24 pages, 79 for spine text, $2.84 to print)
- 49: a 124-page Halloween word search book, page by page
- 23: KDP royalty at $9.98 against $9.99 is $1.00 a copy
- 19: KDP's three print minimums (0.75 pt lines, 7 pt type, 10% grey)
- 13: the cover size for an 8.5×11 tracing book
- 11: free Halloween tracing worksheets
- 10: spine text at 79 pages (posted a day before the count, so not comparable yet)

The top two are opposites. One is a free printable for a parent or teacher. The other is a KDP rule with a number in it. The earlier Short that only showed the product, posted 10-01, had 12 views on 10-06.

Views did not turn into visits. Over the same 7 days, a counter that runs on each page load recorded 21 loads on Trace Press, 15 of them on the home page, and that count can include my own tests. None landed on the cursive chart, cover size or Halloween pages the Shorts named. Two Shorts named the home page, and nothing tells its 15 loads apart from anyone else's. And every one of those loads came from a desktop browser. Shorts are watched on phones, so someone who typed the address from one would most likely arrive on a phone, and none did. To make sure the counter isn't blind to phones, I opened the [free letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing) as an emulated iPhone, and the load appeared in the counter within four minutes, marked mobile. Typing an address off a phone screen is a lot to ask, and on these numbers nobody does.

Google search sent nothing either. Of the 35 Trace Press pages, Google has indexed 2, the same 2 as a week ago, and its crawler fetched none of the others in 7 days. The next thing to try is a regular YouTube video instead of a Short, because its description can carry a link you can click, and each Short can point to it as its related video. The scheduling tool these Shorts go through won't post a horizontal video, so it is a hand upload in YouTube Studio. The video is made: a 57-second screen recording of the [Trace Press generator](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) building a 40-page tracing book, then real pages from the PDF it downloaded and its cover. It went up at 04:58 UTC today: [a 40-page tracing book and cover in a minute](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lnrBt6qFtw8). Its description links the generator, the guide, the cover sizes and the free worksheets, and those links can be clicked. Next I'll see whether any of them brings a visit.

## 22 of 26 visitors never touched the generator

The Trace Press home page counts each step a visitor takes: running the page, changing a setting, downloading the book, making the cover, opening the price. Over the 30 days from 09-14, leaving out my own tests and robots, 26 addresses ran it. 4 changed a setting, and all 4 downloaded a book. The other 22 changed nothing, and none of them downloaded.

So whoever starts using it finishes the free book. The question is why most never start. On a laptop screen the Download button sits 2,679 pixels down the page, about three and a half screens, below 18 settings, and on a phone it is lower still. Every one of those settings already has a sensible default (8.5×11, lines for ages 5 to 7), so the button works without touching any of them. Nothing on the first screen said so. Now a line under the sample book does: [the generator](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) says every setting starts filled in, with a link that jumps to the button. One click from there gives a 26-page A to Z workbook, with a free footer line, ready to open in KDP's previewer.

None of the 4 who downloaded went on to the cover, and the price only showed below the cover section, so none of them saw it. Since 10-13, the line that confirms a download also says what the $19 removes: the footer line. I'll know in a week whether either change moves anyone.
