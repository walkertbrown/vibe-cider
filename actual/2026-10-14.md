## The numbers people search KDP's help pages for

Google's Search Console shows the searches that put Puzzle Press in front of someone. Over the last 30 days, some of them were people trying to find one number inside Amazon's own help pages: `site:kdp.amazon.com "create a paperback cover" "0.002252"`, `site:kdp.amazon.com "0.0025" cream paper spine width`, `site:kdp.amazon.com "151 to 300" "0.5" margins`. They already half-knew the figure and wanted to confirm it. Here are those figures in one place, as Puzzle Press uses them:

| Paper (black & white unless noted) | Thickness per page | Spine at 120 pages |
|---|---|---|
| White | 0.002252" | 0.270" (6.86 mm) |
| Cream | 0.0025" | 0.300" (7.62 mm) |
| Groundwood | 0.00235" | 0.282" (7.16 mm) |
| Premium colour | 0.002347" | 0.282" |
| Standard colour | 0.002252" | 0.270" |

Groundwood is the one KDP's help pages leave out. Puzzle Press got 0.00235" from KDP's own cover calculator: a 1.946" spine at 828 pages.

The inside margin (gutter) KDP requires grows with the page count: 0.375" up to 150 pages, 0.5" for 151 to 300, 0.625" to 500, 0.75" to 700 and 0.875" to 828. The outside, top and bottom margins are 0.25", or 0.375" with bleed. These are minimums: more is fine, less gets the file rejected.

## Three first moves start 12 lowercase letters

If you're making a lowercase tracing book, the order of the pages can teach something. I grouped the 26 lowercase letters in Trace Press by the first thing the pencil does, using the same stroke data the worksheets are drawn from:

- **A circle**, counterclockwise from the right: a, d, g, q. After the circle, the pencil goes up and down the right side. Only the length of that line changes: it stops at the baseline for a, goes up to the top line first for d, and drops below the baseline for g and q.
- **A tall line**, top to bottom: b, h, k, l.
- **A short line**, from the middle line down: i, m, n, r.

That's 12 of 26. It also shows why b and d are different to write, not just mirror images: b starts with the line and d starts with the circle.

These are Trace Press's letter shapes. School letter models differ, so compare them with the one your school uses. Every page of the free [lowercase letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/lowercase-letter-tracing) has a numbered dot where each stroke starts and an arrow for the way it goes. There are 26 pages, a to z.

## A free KDP cover template for any trim, page count and paper

The [KDP spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) now has a button under the canvas size: **Download this cover template (PNG)**. It draws the full-wrap cover at 300 DPI for the trim, page count and paper you picked. A 6 × 9 book of 120 pages on cream paper gives 3765 × 2775 pixels. On it:

- **Pink:** the 0.125" bleed on every outside edge, which is trimmed off. Run background art right to the edge.
- **A red line** for the trim and **two dark lines** for the spine folds.
- **Blue dashes:** a box 0.125" inside the trim on the front and back. KDP asks for text at least that far inside the trim lines, and the box keeps the same distance from the folds. From 79 pages, when KDP allows spine text, there's a second box on the spine, 0.0625" in from each fold. That's the spine safe area KDP's own cover calculator gives.
- **The barcode box:** 2" × 1.2" in the lower right of the back cover, 0.25" in from the bottom and from the spine. That's the area KDP's downloadable templates keep clear.

Put it in Canva, Photoshop or Affinity as the bottom layer, design over it, then hide it before you export the PDF. It's made in your browser from the same numbers Puzzle Press builds its own covers with, so it matches the cover the generator would make for that book.

## Free KDP interior page templates, odd and even

The cover now has a template, so the pages get one too. The [KDP margin calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/margin-calculator) has two buttons under its facing-pages diagram: **Odd-page template** and **Even-page template**. Each one is a PNG at 300 DPI, exactly the size of the PDF page KDP expects for your trim, page count and bleed setting.

For a 6 × 9 book of 120 pages:

| | No bleed | With bleed |
|---|---|---|
| Template size | 1800 × 2700 px | 1838 × 2775 px |
| Gutter (inside margin) | 0.375" | 0.375" |
| Outside margin, from the page edge | 0.25" | 0.5" |
| Top and bottom, from the page edge | 0.25" | 0.5" |

With bleed, the outside, top and bottom margins are 0.375" from the trim plus the 0.125" bleed.

What's on each page:

- **Blue dashes:** KDP's minimum margins. Keep text inside them.
- **Shaded strip:** the gutter. It's on the left of odd pages (1, 3, 5…, the right-hand pages) and on the right of even ones. Putting it on the same side of every page is one of the mistakes that gets an interior rejected, and it's the easiest one to make when you design one page at a time.
- **Pink (with bleed on):** the extra 0.125". It goes on the outside edge, the top and the bottom only, never the gutter side.

Put the matching template under each page as the bottom layer, then hide it before you export. The margins come from the same function the Puzzle Press generator lays out its own pages with.
