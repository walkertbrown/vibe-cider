# Actual — 2026-10-18

## A paperback spine has nothing added to it

Some KDP spine calculators add 0.06" to the spine. KDP's own paperback cover help adds nothing. Spine width is the page count times the paper's thickness:

- white paper: 0.002252" a page
- cream paper: 0.0025" a page
- premium colour: 0.002347" a page

I re-read that page today to be sure. The 0.06" is a hardcover allowance. Put it on a paperback and your cover comes out 0.06" wider than KDP expects.

A worked example: a 6" × 9" paperback of 100 pages on cream paper.

- The spine is 100 × 0.0025" = 0.25".
- The full cover is 0.125" bleed + 6" back + 0.25" spine + 6" front + 0.125" bleed = 12.5" wide, by 9" + 0.25" = 9.25" tall.

Spine text has its own limit. KDP's help says "at least 79 pages" in one place, and Cover Creator's help says 80. A book KDP counted at 79 pages had its spine text refused, so plan on 80. Even at 80 pages on cream, the strip you can print on is 0.075" wide once 0.0625" is kept clear at each fold. That is under 5 pt of type.

The [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) works this out for any trim, paper and page count, and draws a 300 DPI cover template with the folds and the barcode box marked.

## The gutter grows in steps

The inside margin (the gutter) depends on the page count, and it doesn't grow smoothly. It jumps at fixed counts. These are from KDP's help, re-read today:

| Pages | Inside margin (gutter) |
|---|---|
| 24–150 | 0.375" |
| 151–300 | 0.5" |
| 301–500 | 0.625" |
| 501–700 | 0.75" |
| 701–828 | 0.875" |

The outside, top and bottom margins are at least 0.25" without bleed and 0.375" with it, whatever the page count.

So one extra page can move the gutter. Adding a page to a 150-page book takes it from 0.375" to 0.5", and every page needs reflowing. Check the page count you'll end with before you set margins. The [margin calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/margin-calculator) shows the gutter, the margins and the PDF page size for any page count, with or without bleed.

## A tracing book cover is not 17" × 11"

An 8.5" × 11" tracing workbook sounds like it needs a cover twice as wide: 17" × 11". It doesn't. A 26-page book on white paper needs 17.309" × 11.25":

- 0.125" of bleed on each outside edge: 0.25" across and 0.25" down
- a spine of 26 × 0.002252" = 0.059"

The [tracing book cover size](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-book-cover-size) page lists the cover for 26 to 78 pages in all six trims, on white and cream paper. The free 26-page sample cover it links to measures exactly that size.
