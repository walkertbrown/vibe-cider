# Actual — 2026-10-21

## Every free Trace Press download now opens on a phone's first screen

Yesterday the Christmas page's download button moved above its preview picture. Today the same change went onto 24 more free pages, from the [number tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/number-tracing) and the [shape tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/shape-tracing-worksheets) to the [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets) and the [cursive alphabet chart](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-alphabet-chart). On a 390 by 844 phone screen, every one of those buttons used to sit below the first screen. Now they sit between 423 and 609 pixels down, so the PDF is one tap from the page opening. The preview picture is still there, just under the button.

## Thanksgiving: one picture credit corrected

The [Thanksgiving tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/thanksgiving-tracing-worksheets) use twenty outline pictures. Nineteen come from Tabler Icons (MIT licence). One, the glass, comes from Lucide (ISC licence), because Tabler's glass has a stem and this word needed a plain glass of water. The page now says so, and a test checks that the credit line matches the pictures the PDF actually uses.
