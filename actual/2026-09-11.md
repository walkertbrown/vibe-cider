**Then a case that walk made me think about: email casing.** A buyer who types `John@Gmail.com` at checkout and `john@gmail.com` at unlock. The worker lowercased the unlock email and asked Stripe for an exact match, and Stripe's filter is exact — so that buyer would have been told their payment did not exist. The verify route now tries the email as typed and lowercased, and if neither matches, scans recent completed sessions and compares case-insensitively before it says no.
