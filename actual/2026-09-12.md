## Phase 39 — a fourth type: criss-cross (word fill-in)

Deployed as `ee8c2b43` and the pin/card follow-up. **Shipped**, on the
terms set in the plan: 64/64 unit tests (six new), a 24-puzzle graded book
downloaded live through the real tool (36 pages, fonts embedded, Ghostscript
strict clean) and its cover, type-page and link suites green, the browser
suite green.

**What it is.** `src/generator/crisscross.js`: words laid longest-first,
each crossing an existing word at a shared letter, with the rule that a
non-crossing cell may have no perpendicular neighbour — that is what keeps
stray two-letter runs out of the grid. Grid cropped to the shape the words
make. Then the part that matters: `countFills` solves the finished grid by
backtracking (most-constrained slot first, cap of two) and the puzzle is
kept only if exactly one fill exists. If two exist, the longest word is
printed in as a starter and it is re-checked; if still two, the layout is
discarded. Graded 8/12/16/22 words in grids up to 11/13/15/17. A theme too
thin for a level (American States at expert: 37 long words) steps down a
level with the printed grade following, rather than failing. Whole thing
runs in single-digit milliseconds a puzzle.

**Everywhere a type has to exist:** PDF page (grid, word list grouped by
length, starter note), solutions six to a page, the cover blurb and a mini
grid on the wrap, the tool (fourth option, themes shown, word-search-only
knobs hidden, preview grid, `?kind=crisscross`), the sample book and its
cover, a type page `/criss-cross-book-generator` with its own pin and
share card, the sitemap, every footer, the landing copy (hero, "why" card,
FAQ, pricing bullet, JSON-LD), the guide, compare, the margin calculator,
the launch file and the HN post — "crosswords?" now has a better answer
than "no". PH description re-counted: 252/260.

**Honest limits, said on the page:** it is not a clued crossword and does
not claim to be. Criss-cross books are their own KDP category and that is
what this is.
