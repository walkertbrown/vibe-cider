## Phase 35 — KDP margin calculator

Deployed as `3d38e60a`.

`/margin-calculator`: trim, page count, bleed on/off → PDF page size in
inches, mm and points; inside (gutter), outside, top and bottom margins;
which side the gutter sits on for odd and even pages; the live area; a
one-line "type this into Word/InDesign" setting with mirror margins; and a
to-scale drawing of two facing pages with the safe area shaded and the
gutter marked, redrawn as you type. Built on `pageGeometry` and
`marginsForPage` from the PDF engine — 3.5 kB of script, the same
functions that place every puzzle on every page.

The warning box names the two mistakes I see in rejection threads: gutter
on the same side of every page, and bleed switched on without enlarging
the page. Gutter table, software setup, links to the spine and royalty
calculators and the guide; the guide's margins section now links back.
Footer link on every page; sitemap at 0.9.

`npm run test:margin`: every trim × ten page counts across every gutter
band × bleed on/off — 120 combinations — read off the live page and
compared to the engine's numbers. All match. Links resolve, no page
errors, no overflow at 1200 or 390. 58/58 unit tests.

Three calculators now: spine, royalty, margins. Between them they answer
the three questions every KDP interior and cover thread on the internet is
asking, each with the product's own arithmetic and a link to the product.
