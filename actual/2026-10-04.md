## Knowing when a free worksheet gets downloaded

Every free worksheet on Trace Press is a plain PDF link, from the
[Halloween tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets)
to the [preschool pack](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/preschool-tracing-worksheets).
In the server log, a parent who clicks one looks just like a search engine
fetching it.

So the 30 pages with a free PDF now carry one line of script. When a person
clicks a PDF link, the browser asks for a blank one-pixel image, /px/pdf.gif,
and that request is the whole message. There's no cookie or ID, and nothing
from the page is sent. Crawlers fetch the PDF without clicking, so they don't
fire it.

It went in before the Halloween YouTube Short posts at 00:30Z. The Short
points at the Halloween page, so the beacon is how I'll tell whether anyone
it sends goes on to take the worksheets.

## A copyright page, if you want one

Until now a Trace Press interior had no copyright page, so you had to add
one in another program. [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/)
can now put one in the interior itself: tick "Copyright page" and it comes right
after the "This book belongs to" page, or first if you don't have one.
That puts it on the back of the name page.

It has three lines, small and low on the page: "Copyright ©" and the year,
the author you typed for the cover, and "All rights reserved." A long
author name wraps rather than running off the page. A test checks every
line against the real embedded font on all six trims, with and without
bleed. It's one of the 52 extra pages, so the book stays under the 79-page
mark where KDP starts allowing spine text.

## Page numbers, if you want them

The [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) interior can
now print page numbers. Each one is small (9pt), at the bottom outside
corner: right on odd pages, left on even, which are the outside edges of a
bound book. The numbers count every page, including the title, copyright
and "This book belongs to" pages, so page 12 in the book is page 12 in
KDP's page count and in your PDF viewer. Those front pages carry no number
themselves. Numbers add no pages.

They sit level with the free preview's footer line. A test measures both
in the real embedded font on every page of the largest book, on all six
trims with and without bleed, and checks that they never touch. Turning
numbers on doesn't change the rest of the layout, so the
[how-to guide](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-handwriting-workbook)
still applies.

## Three KDP numbers for a tracing book, as a Short

A new Trace Press YouTube Short is scheduled for 00:30Z on 10-05. It gives
three numbers to know before making an alphabet or tracing book on KDP.

- **24 pages** is KDP's shortest paperback. One page a letter, A to Z, is
  26, so an alphabet book clears it.
- **79 pages** is where KDP starts allowing text on the spine. A 26-page book
  on white paper has a spine 0.0586" thick, so it stays blank.
- **$2.84** is KDP's printing cost for black ink at 8.5x11 on Amazon.com, and
  it's the same for every book from 24 to 110 pages. So pages past 26 cost
  nothing more to print until 110. At 6x9 it's $2.30.

The video doesn't have the numbers typed into it. The script that makes it
imports them from the same code that sizes Trace Press books and covers. The
printing cost comes from KDP's "Paperback Printing Cost" help page, and the
spine figures from "Create a Paperback Cover". Every page shown is a real page
of the free [A–Z sample workbook](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/letter-tracing-workbook-sample-8.5x11.pdf)
and its cover. Cover widths for each page count are on the
[tracing book cover size](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-book-cover-size)
page.

## Capital and lowercase on one page: what changes between them

The free [letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing)
put the capital and the lowercase letter on the same page. That page now
describes each pair.

For seven letters, the lowercase is the capital at half the height, drawn
with the same strokes in the same order and direction: c, o, s, v, w, x and
z. The other 19 change shape between the cases. For example, u gains a line
down its right side, and the lowercase b, d, p and q are each one stroke
where the capital takes two.

Each green number on a page is one stroke, so a page takes from 2 to 5:

- Two: Cc, Gg, Ll, Oo, Ss, Uu, Vv, Ww and Zz.
- Three: Bb, Dd, Mm, Nn, Pp, Qq and Rr.
- Four: Aa, Hh, Jj, Kk, Tt, Xx and Yy.
- Five: Ee, Ff and Ii.

The lists are read from the letter shapes Trace Press draws, and a test checks
the page against them. For one case per page there are the
[uppercase](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/uppercase-letter-tracing)
and [lowercase](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/lowercase-letter-tracing)
sets, each with its own stroke and height lists.

The back of a Trace Press cover used to be plain. Now there's an optional

seven keywords or short phrases". It asks you to leave out words already in
your title, claims like "best", words like "new", quotation marks, and brands

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now lists seven
phrases under the listing description, each with its own Copy button. They

out, and the next one moves up. There are no ages or claims about the child,
only what's on the pages.

If you're still working out the trim size, the
[cover size table](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-book-cover-size)

## Sofía, not Sofa

The free [name tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing)
sheet left out any letter it had no strokes for. "Sofía" printed as "Sofa".
"José" lost its é and "Zoë" lost its ë. The page said accented letters
weren't drawn yet, but a name sheet that misspells the name isn't usable.

Accented letters now trace as they're spelled: á é í ó ú ý, à è ì ò ù,
â ê î ô û, ä ë ï ö ü ÿ, ã õ ñ, and å, in capitals too. Each is drawn as its
plain letter, then the accent as one more stroke from the top, with its own
start dot. On a lowercase letter the accent sits where the dot of an i goes.
On a capital it sits just above the top line, in the space between rows.

The same letters work on the
[cursive name sheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-name-tracing),
in the [word tracing tool](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-worksheet-generator)
(piñata, café), and on word pages inside a book. Hyphens, apostrophes, ç and ø
still aren't drawn. The note under the name box says when something was left
out.

## Mary-Kate, O'Neil, François

The [name tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing)
sheet dropped hyphens and apostrophes, so "Mary-Kate" printed as "MaryKate"
and "O'Neil" as "ONeil". The ç was dropped too, so "François" came out as
"Franois". All three trace now.

A hyphen is one stroke, left to right, across the middle of the lowercase
letters. An apostrophe is one short stroke down from the top line. A curly
apostrophe, which is what phones type, is made straight. The ç is a c, then
a cedilla as a second stroke: it starts at the bottom of the c, drops below
the line and hooks back to the left, with its own start dot.

This works on the
[cursive name sheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-name-tracing),
in the [word tracing tool](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-worksheet-generator)
(don't, garçon), and on word pages inside a book. ø and other punctuation
aren't drawn yet, and the note under the box still says when something was
left out.
