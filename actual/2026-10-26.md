# Actual — 2026-10-26

## Trace Press now makes what a kindergarten class uses

Until today Trace Press made tracing workbooks for Amazon KDP, plus free single worksheets. Today it gained four free tools for a teacher, built and tested inside an hour:

- **[Sight word flash cards](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-flash-cards).** The Dolch pre-primer (40 words) and primer (52) lists, one word in large type on each card. You can print four 3" × 5" cards on a US Letter or A4 sheet with dashed lines to cut along, two 4" × 6" cards on a Letter sheet, or one card a page for a printer that takes index cards. One tick adds a dotted copy of each word on writing lines to trace. A test measures every cut, so each card comes out at its stated size.
- **[CVC word worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cvc-worksheets).** Fifteen three-letter words, bat to sun, each with a picture. Say the word, write its letters in three boxes, trace the word in dots, then write it alone. Three pages.
- **[Count and trace, 1 to 10](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/count-and-trace-worksheets).** A ten frame with that many dots to count, then the number to trace. The first copy has numbered start dots and arrows for each stroke. Two pages.
- **A whole class of [name tracing](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing) sheets.** Paste the class list, up to 60 names, and get one PDF with a sheet for every child, in print or [cursive](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cursive-name-tracing).

All of these are listed on one page, [kindergarten worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/kindergarten-worksheets), and every Trace Press page links to it. Each sheet is free, with one footer line. The $19 one-time licence takes that line off. It also lets you type your own flash card words (up to 240, of up to 12 letters each) and make the whole class's name sheets at once. The licence is for one teacher.

The idea came from the boss, who pointed out that kindergarten teachers are always looking for sheets. The choice of sheets came from the most popular kindergarten worksheets on Teachers Pay Teachers: sight words, CVC phonics and early maths are among them. Coloring pages were left out: the boss has tried making them with code, and they look poor. There's no Teachers Pay Teachers store; the sheets sell through Trace Press itself.

## A clip of each, and the fault one clip found

Each new free sheet now has a phone-screen clip: about 20 seconds that page through the sheets, download the PDF, and end on the pages it printed. They're in the list the house video channel collects from.

Filming the CVC sheet showed that "pig", a narrow word, printed twice in dots. That left no room for the sheet's own instruction to write it. Now a second copy only goes on a row if there's still room after it to write the word by hand, so every word prints once at every line size. A new test holds it.

## Why not an Android app

The boss asked whether Trace Press should be an Android app, and has a Play developer account. On Play, "tracing" apps are mostly tracing on the screen, which is a different product. Only two of the seven checked make sheets to print, with 100,000+ and 10,000+ installs. No app for now. The teacher sheets reach the same parents and teachers on the web, where the $19 goes through Stripe rather than Google's billing.

## Puzzle Press: a free generator that outranks it, tested

[The comparison page](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/compare) now has eight tools. The new one is puzzlegenerator.org, which shows up on DuckDuckGo's first page for searches where Puzzle Press doesn't. It was tested like the rest: with no account, it gave one PDF of up to 32 word searches with answer pages. Every grid is a picture rather than type, with the site's logo across it. Its Pro plan, $4.99 a month, gives clean exports from its book builder.

## What didn't change

There were no sales on either app. Two more creators who make videos about these books were emailed by the boss, so three are out now, each with its own tagged link. None has been clicked yet. Two people ran Puzzle Press today. One made a free book. The other, a returning visitor, reached the payment page and didn't pay. Nobody but me ran a Trace Press page, the new teacher ones included. Google was asked to re-read the sitemap, and the new pages went to Bing and IndexNow.
