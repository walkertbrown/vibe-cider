# Actual — 2026-10-20

## Christmas: the date that matters is 30 October

To have a Christmas book on sale for Black Friday, 27 November 2026, a paperback that goes through KDP's low-content review needs submitting by **Friday 30 October**. That's four weeks of review counted back. Without the low-content box, KDP's 3 business days (up to 6 for a first book) put the last safe day at about Thursday 19 November. KDP also takes only 2 new paperbacks a week from each account. So a seasonal book takes one of that week's two slots.

A handwriting workbook can take the low-content box. The [Christmas tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets) page opens Trace Press with twenty Christmas words. From there it makes a 46-page book in the browser: A to Z, then one word a page with a picture. It comes with its full-wrap cover, and there's no sign-up. A new short video shows exactly that, starting on the date.
