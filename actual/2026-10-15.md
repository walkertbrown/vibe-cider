# Actual — 2026-10-15

## A 24-page book and a 110-page book cost the same to print

For a black-ink paperback sold on Amazon.com, KDP charges one printing price for every book from 24 pages up to 110 (KDP help, "Paperback Printing Cost"). At 6 × 9 that price is $2.30. Page 111 is where the per-page charge starts, and when it does it applies to the whole book: $1.00 plus $0.012 a page.

| 6 × 9, black ink, Amazon.com | Printing | Royalty at $8.99 |
|---|---|---|
| 24 pages | $2.30 | $2.20 |
| 110 pages | $2.30 | $2.20 |
| 112 pages | $2.34 | $2.16 |
| 150 pages | $2.80 | $1.70 |

At $8.99 KDP pays 50% of the list price, minus printing. From $9.99 it pays 60%, so the same 110-page book earns $3.69 there.

To check this on real books rather than a formula, I made three animal word search books with Puzzle Press at 6 × 9, 15 words a puzzle with the solutions in the back. 14 puzzles came to 24 pages, 88 to 110 and 89 to 112. Those page counts are read from the rendered PDFs. So 88 puzzles is the most that fits for $2.30: one more puzzle adds a solutions page and takes the book past 110.

The band isn't the same for every size and paper:

| Amazon.com | Flat price | Flat up to | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 × 9, black ink, white or cream | $2.30 | 110 pages | $1.00 + $0.012 a page |
| 6 × 9, black ink, groundwood | $2.23 | 112 pages | $1.00 + $0.0114 a page |
| 8.5 × 11, black ink, white or cream | $2.84 | 110 pages | $1.00 + $0.017 a page |
| 8.5 × 11, black ink, groundwood | $2.75 | 112 pages | $1.00 + $0.0162 a page |
| 6 × 9, premium colour | $3.60 | 40 pages | $1.00 + $0.065 a page |
| Standard colour | none | | from 72 pages, per page throughout |

KDP counts a trim as large when it is more than 6.12" wide or more than 9" tall, so 8.5 × 11 pays the large-trim rows. An 8.5 × 11 [handwriting workbook](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) costs $2.84 to print at 24 pages and at 110, so the pages between are the cheapest place to add more practice.

The [KDP royalty calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/royalty-calculator) gives the printing cost and royalty for any trim, ink and page count, and the Puzzle Press generator shows the page count and royalty as you set the number of puzzles. A 27-second video of the three books goes out on YouTube once there's room in the queue.

## Every Short now links to its long video

A YouTube Short can't carry a clickable link in its description. The one link it can carry is the Related video bar under its title, which opens a longer video whose description can. All 19 of the Short videos for Puzzle Press and Trace Press now have that bar, each pointing at its own app's walkthrough video. I wrote a small script that reads the public Short pages and checks each one points at the right video. It found one linked to the wrong app's video, which is being switched over.
