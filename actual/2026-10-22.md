# Actual — 2026-10-22

## The sudoku generator, measured on 2,400 puzzles

The [sudoku book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sudoku-book-generator) page now gives numbers for what the generator does. It ran 200 times for each of the four grades at 9×9, 6×6 and 4×4, and every result was solved again from scratch. All 2,400 puzzles had exactly one solution.

The cost of that check grows with the grade. On easy, 6% of the clue pairs the generator tries to remove have to go back in, because without them the puzzle would have a second answer. On expert, 65% go back. That is why a 9×9 expert puzzle takes 141 ms in the middle case and up to 1.9 seconds, against about 1 ms for easy. Some grids can't be dug down to 26 symmetric clues at all. For 7 of the 200 expert puzzles, the closest the generator found was 27, and the book keeps that 27-clue puzzle rather than one that solves two ways.

## The maze generator, measured on 2,000 mazes

The [maze book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/maze-book-generator) page now has its own numbers. 500 mazes were generated at each grade, and every one was checked: every cell is reachable, and there is exactly one route from entrance to exit. One finding is useful to anyone making a graded book. Size alone does not set difficulty. The longest easy maze had a 179-cell route, longer than the shortest hard maze's 105. So before you publish, page through the preview. If an early maze looks too long for its place, Reshuffle remakes the whole book.

## Criss-cross: a Medium book now says when a puzzle came out Easy

Measuring the [criss-cross book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/criss-cross-book-generator) turned up a fault. A puzzle that can't be built at the grade you asked for drops one grade rather than leave a gap in the book. That part is fine. But it happened silently. A Medium criss-cross book of West Virginia words came out as twenty Easy grids, with nothing printed to say so. Across 90 themes, 209 of 1,800 Medium puzzles had dropped a grade this way.

Now each of those puzzles is labelled with its real level, and the tool says how many there are before you download. You can paste in a longer word list, or knowingly accept the easier grade. Crossword books had the same fallback and got the same fix. The other half of the measurement was clean: all 1,800 grids, re-solved from scratch, had exactly one way to fill them.

## Crosswords: how much of the grid is crossed

The [crossword book generator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/crossword-book-generator) page now has its numbers too: 7,200 puzzles, every answer with its clue. In these themed grids, the middle puzzle crosses only about one letter in seven with another answer. A dense grid crosses every square. So here the clues do the work, and every clue is written in plain language for that reason. The level warning added to criss-cross books this morning covers crossword books too. A third of Medium themes had been quietly giving some Easy grids.
