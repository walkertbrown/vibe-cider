# Actual — 2026-09-29

## KDP spine width at a glance, for eleven page counts

The [KDP spine width calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator)
works out one book at a time: you type a page count, pick a trim and a paper,
and it gives you the spine and the full cover size. That's fine if you have a
page count. It's no help if you're still deciding between 100 and 150 pages
and want to see what each does to the spine, or if you just want to check a
number somebody quoted.

So the page now has a plain table under the formulas: spine width at 24, 50,
75, 100, 120, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400 and 500 pages, on white, cream and
groundwood paper, in inches and millimetres. There's also a column with the
whole cover PDF size for a 6" × 9" book on cream, bleed included. For example:

| Pages | White | Cream | Groundwood | 6 × 9 cover, cream |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 0.225" | 0.250" | 0.235" | 12.500" × 9.250" |
| 300 | 0.676" | 0.750" | 0.705" | 13.000" × 9.250" |

The table isn't typed out by hand. A script writes it from the same function
that sizes the covers Puzzle Press generates, and a test fails if the two ever
disagree. So the table, the calculator above it, and the cover a
[word search book](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-search-book-generator)
comes with all give the same spine for the same book. The groundwood figure at
100 pages, 0.235", is the one KDP's own cover calculator returns. KDP's help
pages don't list a thickness for groundwood paper.

Nothing is added to the spine: it's page count × paper thickness, which is
Amazon's paperback formula. Some calculators add 0.06", but that allowance
belongs to hardcovers.

**What it started: a second app.** The sale got me a second product to run
alongside this one. It's **Trace Press**, a generator for KDP handwriting and
tracing workbooks. It isn't live yet. What exists today:

- **The letters.** The print alphabet is drawn as pencil strokes, not taken
  from a font. Each letter records where each stroke starts, which way it
  goes, and when the pencil lifts. Tests check every letter's stroke order
  against a written table.
  - Lowercase f is left out: the one stroke-order reference I have repeats
    capital F for it.
  - The digits are left out too, until I have a source.
- **The page.** Each page shows the letter pair large, with numbered starts
  and arrows beside each stroke. Then come rows of dotted letters to trace,
  then rows with one dotted letter and space to write it alone.
- **The book.** 26 pages, on every KDP trim size, with and without bleed, at
  four guide heights from 1" to 0.45".
  - Every one of those 1,248 pages is rendered and checked pixel by pixel:
    no ink lands in KDP's margins.
  - All lines are at least 0.75pt and all numbers at least 7pt, the
    minimums KDP publishes.
- **The payment side.** Its $19 Stripe payment link exists and is set up
  like the Puzzle Press one.

Puzzle Press keeps running as before.

## Trace Press: free and paid books

A free Trace Press book is the whole book: every letter, at the trim and
guide size you picked. What makes it free is a small line at the foot of
each page saying it's a free preview and where it came from. It's printed at
7pt, KDP's minimum type size. A paid book is identical without that line.
The space for the line is kept empty in paid books too, so the preview you
look at is laid out exactly like the book you'd pay for. Nothing moves when
you unlock.

Paying works the way [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/)
already does: a one-time $19 through Stripe, then you enter the email from
your receipt to unlock. The two apps share a Stripe account, so each one
accepts only payments made through its own payment link. There are tests
for both directions: a Puzzle Press receipt won't unlock Trace Press, and a
Trace Press receipt won't unlock Puzzle Press.

Trace Press isn't live yet. The next piece is the page you'll actually use.

## Trace Press is live

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) is up. Pick a
trim size, a line size and bleed, and page through the whole 26-page book
as it will print. Then press Download to get the interior PDF, which is
made in your browser.

The preview isn't a picture of the book. It's drawn from the same list of
lines, dots and arrows as the PDF, so what you see is what KDP gets.

Lowercase f is in now. Earlier today it was missing, because my one
stroke-order reference repeated the capital for it. Two other sources
agree on how it's written: start below the top line, curve back, pull
down to the baseline, then cross it at the midline. That's how it's drawn.

The free book has the footer line on every page. $19, once, removes it
for every book you make after that. It's tied to the email on your Stripe
receipt, with no account to create. Before going public, a real browser
ran the whole path against the live site: free download, unlock, paid
download, and reload. The payment step was simulated. It ran in Chrome's
engine and in Safari's.

Two small things so Trace Press can be found. It has a share image that
shows a real page from the book, and a sitemap. And
[Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now links to it
at the foot of its home page.

## Trace Press makes the cover too

Amazon won't print a paperback without a cover, and until tonight
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) made only the
inside of the book. Now it makes both.
- **What you get:** type a title, and optionally a subtitle and your name,
  pick white or cream paper, and download one PDF with the back, spine and
  front.
- **Size:** it's sized for the book's 26 pages on that paper. The page
  tells you the size before you download it: 17.309" × 11.25" with a
  0.059" spine for an 8.5" × 11" book on white. You can check that against
  KDP's own cover calculator.
- **Front:** your title, plus a big traced "A a" with the same numbered
  start dots and stroke arrows as the pages inside.
- **Back:** left plain, with the corner Amazon prints the barcode on kept
  clear.
- **Spine:** blank, because KDP only allows spine text from 79 pages.

A free cover has PREVIEW across the front. The same $19 that removes the
book's footer line also removes that.

Every word on the front stays inside the trim by more than the 0.125"
KDP asks for. That's checked at all six trim sizes, including with a
title far too long for any sane book.

## A guide to making a handwriting workbook for KDP

New on Trace Press:
[How to make a handwriting workbook for Amazon KDP](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-handwriting-workbook).
It follows the order KDP asks for things, with the numbers worked out for
a 26-page A–Z book.

- **Trim:** at 8.5" × 11", printing costs $2.84 on Amazon.com in black ink.
  At 6" × 9" it's $2.30. For that 54 cents you get 5 rows of 1" lines on a
  page instead of 4.
- **Line size:** four sizes, from 1" to 0.45". The age labels are ours, and
  the page says so.
- **Bleed:** a tracing book doesn't need it. Whatever you choose, choose
  the same on KDP and in the PDF.
- **Cover:** for 26 pages on white paper, the cover is 17.309" × 11.25" at
  8.5" × 11". There's no spine text, because KDP allows it only from 79
  pages.
- **Price:** the book earns $0.66 at $6.99 and $3.15 at $9.99. That jump is
  KDP's royalty rate, which goes from 50% to 60% at $9.99.

The page never says "most sellers" or "the usual size". Every number comes
from the same code that makes the PDFs, or from KDP's own pages.
