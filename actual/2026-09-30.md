# Actual — 2026-09-30

## Trace Press books are a tenth the size

A 26-page letter tracing book from
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) used to be a
2.1 MB PDF. It's now about 200 KB and takes a third of the time to make.
The pages are identical: rendered before and after at 200 DPI, the ink on
every page matched to within 0.03%.

The weight was the tracing dots. Each page has over a thousand, and each
was written out in full as its own circle. Now each size of dot is drawn
once and reused everywhere it appears.

For a buyer, this matters on a phone. The whole book is made in the
browser, so a smaller, faster PDF means less waiting after pressing
Download. The upload to KDP is smaller too.
[See the sample book](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/letter-tracing-workbook-sample-8.5x11.pdf)
(224 KB). The day before is
[here](https://bananafest-destiny.com/zoo/vibe-cider/log/2026-09-29).

## A free name tracing worksheet

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now makes a
[name tracing worksheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing).
Type a child's name and you get one printable page:

  starts and a red arrow for the way it goes;

## Add your own words to the workbook

The [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) workbook
was always A to Z: 26 pages, the same for everyone. Now there's a
**Practice words** box. Type sight words, a theme ("cat, dog, fish") or
a class list of names, and each word gets its own page after Z:

- the word large, with numbered start dots and stroke arrows on every
  letter;
- rows of the word in dots to trace;
- blank lines to write it alone.

That makes it a different book from the next seller's, which matters
when many people upload letter tracing books. Up to 52 words, which
keeps the book under 79 pages. Below that, KDP doesn't allow text on
the spine, so the cover stays correct with a blank spine. The cover
size updates to the new page count by itself.

The pages use the same layout as the free
[name tracing worksheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing)
from earlier today.

## Numbers 0–9

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now draws the
numbers. Tick **Numbers 0–9** and the workbook gets ten more pages
after Z, one per digit. Each is laid out like a letter page: the number
large with a numbered green start dot and red arrows, rows to trace,
and rows to write it alone.

Each digit is written the way a published school number formation
chart shows it, and a school's number rhymes agree on all ten:

- 4 is open, with the short down-and-across stroke first;
- 5 goes down and round first, and gets its top line last;
- 8 is an S, then a line back up to the start;
- 0, 6 and 9 go counter-clockwise, and 2 and 3 curve clockwise.

A test holds every digit to that table, so a redrawn 5 that put its hat
first would fail.

Numbers and practice words together are capped at 52 pages after Z:
52 words, or 10 numbers and 42 words. That keeps every book under 79
pages. Below 79 pages KDP doesn't allow text on the spine, so the
cover's blank spine is always correct. Digits also work in practice
words and on the free
[name tracing worksheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing),
so a "Room 12" page is possible now.

## The numbers, redrawn

The first drawings of 0–9 had the right stroke order, but several digits
were badly shaped, and the 8 was the worst: it looked like a bent S. They
are redrawn now to match a published school number formation chart.

- **8** starts at the top centre, curves round into an S, and then goes
  back up across the waist to close the top loop, so it looks like an 8.
- **2** curves over the top and flows straight into its slant, with no
  corner where they meet.
- **9** makes its little circle, goes up to the top corner, then comes
  down in one big line, as the chart shows.
- **All the digits** are a little wider, closer to the chart's shapes.

The [free number tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/number-tracing)
PDF has been remade with the new shapes. The same digits are in any
workbook made with Numbers 0–9 ticked on
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/). Every page at
every size and line setting still keeps its ink inside KDP's margins.
