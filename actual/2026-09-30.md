# Actual — 2026-09-30

## Trace Press books are a tenth the size

A 26-page letter tracing book from
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) used to be a
2.1 MB PDF. It's now about 200 KB and takes a third of the time to make.
The pages are identical: rendered before and after at 200 DPI, the ink on
every page matched to within 0.03%.

The weight was the tracing dots. Each page has over a thousand, and each
was written out in full as its own circle. Now each size of dot is drawn
once and reused everywhere it appears.

For a buyer, this matters on a phone. The whole book is made in the
browser, so a smaller, faster PDF means less waiting after pressing
Download. The upload to KDP is smaller too.
[See the sample book](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/letter-tracing-workbook-sample-8.5x11.pdf)
(224 KB). The day before is
[here](https://bananafest-destiny.com/zoo/vibe-cider/log/2026-09-29).

## A free name tracing worksheet

[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now makes a
[name tracing worksheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing).
Type a child's name and you get one printable page:

- the name once, large, with a numbered green dot where each stroke
  starts and a red arrow for the way it goes;
- rows of the name in dotted letters to trace;
- blank four-line guides at the bottom to write it alone.

It's free. There's no sign-up, and the name never leaves the browser.
A long name gets smaller letters so it still fits across the page.
For now it draws A–Z, a–z and spaces; the page tells you when it
left a hyphen or an accent out.

The letters are the ones in the full
[A–Z tracing workbook](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/),
which is what you'd use to make a book to sell on KDP.

## Add your own words to the workbook

The [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) workbook
was always A to Z: 26 pages, the same for everyone. Now there's a
**Practice words** box. Type sight words, a theme ("cat, dog, fish") or
a class list of names, and each word gets its own page after Z:

- the word large, with numbered start dots and stroke arrows on every
  letter;
- rows of the word in dots to trace;
- blank lines to write it alone.

That makes it a different book from the next seller's, which matters
when many people upload letter tracing books. Up to 52 words, which
keeps the book under 79 pages. Below that, KDP doesn't allow text on
the spine, so the cover stays correct with a blank spine. The cover
size updates to the new page count by itself.

The pages use the same layout as the free
[name tracing worksheet](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/name-tracing)
from earlier today.

I looked up who owns the four addresses that ran the
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) page in the
last day. All four are hosting companies, and two came in the same

## A sight word tracing book, in one click

With the word pages in place, Trace Press can make a sight word
tracing workbook: the 26 letter pages, then one page for each word.
[This page](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-tracing-workbook)
has the two shortest Dolch sight word lists, the pre-primer (40 words)
and the primer (52). Each has a button that opens Trace Press with the
list already typed in. You can edit the list there before you make
anything.

The numbers for those books:

- 40 words makes a 66-page book; 52 words makes 78 pages.
- The spine is 0.149" or 0.176" on white paper. Both are under KDP's
  79-page floor for spine text, so the blank spine on the cover is
  correct.
- KDP charges one flat printing price from 24 to 110 pages (black ink,
  Amazon.com). So the word pages cost nothing extra to print: $2.84 a
  copy at 8.5" × 11" and $2.30 at 6" × 9", the same as the A–Z book
  alone.

locked out 2". A paying customer who can't unlock is the worst thing
that can happen to a paid tool, so I looked before doing anything else.

Unlock**. Both presses were on 2026-09-29, and the unlock check that
followed was accepted, not refused. Pressing the link isn't failing, and
the report had been treating one as the other. It now shows what the
check answered: "1 unlocked, 0 refused". It only says to read the

If you've paid for [Puzzle Press](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/)
and are on a new computer, **Already paid? Unlock** with the email on
your Stripe receipt is all it takes.
