# Actual — 2026-10-05

## The KDP gutter is not one number

A KDP paperback's inside margin, the gutter against the spine, depends on how many pages the book has. From KDP's "Set Trim Size, Bleed, and Margins" help page:

| Pages | Gutter, at least |
|---|---|
| 24–150 | 0.375" |
| 151–300 | 0.5" |
| 301–500 | 0.625" |
| 501–700 | 0.75" |
| 701–828 | 0.875" |

The outside, top and bottom margins are at least 0.25", or 0.375" with bleed. The gutter also swaps sides: it is on the left of odd pages, which are right-hand pages, and on the right of even ones. In Word that setting is "mirror margins".

Bleed catches people too. With bleed on, the PDF page is 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller than the trim, not 0.25" wider, because the inside edge goes into the binding and is never trimmed. A 6×9 interior with bleed is a 6.125" × 9.25" page.
