## Capitals alone, or lowercase alone

Until today every Trace Press page put a letter's capital and lowercase
side by side. Plenty of teachers and parents want one at a time: Google's
own suggestions for "letter tracing" split into "uppercase letter
tracing worksheets" and "lowercase letter tracing worksheets", and one
of them is "lowercase letter tracing with arrows", which is what these
pages have.

So [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now has a
**Letters** setting: capital and lowercase, capitals only, or lowercase
only. It's still 26 pages either way, one letter to a page, with the
same start dots and arrows, and it works with the pre-writing lines,
numbers and practice words. Two new free PDFs come from it:

- [Uppercase letter tracing worksheets, A to Z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/uppercase-letter-tracing)
- [Lowercase letter tracing worksheets, a to z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/lowercase-letter-tracing)

Both use 1" lines, the setting for ages 4–5. I looked at all 52 pages
before they went up. If you sell on KDP, a capitals-only book and a
lowercase-only book are two separate listings, made with the same
settings you already use for the combined book.

## A free "This book belongs to" page, in every KDP size

"This book belongs to page" is a common search on Google. Its suggested
endings include "template", "for kids", "design" and "printable". The
people searching include KDP publishers finishing a children's book, and
Trace Press could already draw the page. So it's now free on its own:
[a "This book belongs to" page](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/this-book-belongs-to-page),
one PDF for each of the six KDP paperback trim sizes, from 5" × 8" to
[8.5" × 11"](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/samples/this-book-belongs-to-page-8.5x11.pdf).

Each page has nothing on it but the words and a big four-line writing
guide for the name: no logo, no footer, no watermark. It's meant to be
dropped into someone else's book, and a footer would make it useless
for that. The font is embedded, the page is the exact trim size, and
everything sits inside KDP's margins for a book of up to 150 pages. A
test checks all six files for size, page count and text. The page links
to [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) with the
name page already ticked, for anyone who wants the whole tracing book.
