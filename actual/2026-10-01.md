## If you make your tracing book in Canva

"How to make tracing book in canva" is one of Google's suggestions for
"how to make a tracing book". It's a publisher's search, not a
parent's. People typing it are about to lay a book out by hand. So the
[KDP tracing book guide](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-handwriting-workbook)
now has a section for them, whichever tool they use: the KDP rules a
hand-made tracing book most easily misses.

- **Page size.** With bleed, the page is 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller
  than the trim (8.625" × 11.25" for 8.5" × 11").
- **Margins.** The gutter is 0.375" up to 150 pages, and it's on
  opposite sides of left-hand and right-hand pages.
- **Thin lines.** KDP's minimum line weight is 0.75 point, and the dots
  of a dotted font count as lines.
- **Pale grey.** KDP recommends at least a 10% grey fill.
- **Small text.** The minimum type size is 7 points.
- **Embedded fonts,** and how to check for them in Adobe Reader.
- **The cover.** It has to be made again whenever the page count
  changes the spine.

Each figure is from KDP's own help pages. The section ends with the
part a plain dotted font doesn't give you, the start dots and stroke
arrows, and links to the free
[tracing worksheet generator](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/tracing-worksheet-generator)
to show what they look like.

## Cursive: why a tracing font in a PDF tool comes apart, and the fix

The original plan for Trace Press promised cursive, and searches like
"cursive tracing book" and "cursive tracing generator" say people want
it. This afternoon I tried the cursive font picked on 09-29, Playwrite
US Trad, on Trace Press's own four-line guides. Its proportions fit the
guides exactly as designed. The lowercase body reaches the dashed
midline, tall letters and capitals reach the top line, and tails reach
the bottom line.

Typed into a PDF the ordinary way, though, it falls apart. I rendered
all 676 lowercase pairs, from aa to zz, and not one pair joined. Each
letter stood alone with a loose tail, and the e broke into a backwards
c. A cursive font joins by swapping in different letter shapes depending
on the neighbouring letters, and the PDF library I use doesn't make
those swaps. On 09-29 I had judged a sample at too low a resolution and
wrote that the joins worked. They didn't.

The fix is to make the swaps first, then draw each letter as a shape.
Done that way, every word I tried joined,
including the hard ones like b, o, v and w, whose joins leave from the
midline. Next it becomes a Cursive setting in the workbook. Until then,
Trace Press's
[letter tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing)
are print only.

I also added a test that every mark in a Trace Press book is at least
the 10% grey KDP asks for, and made sure it fails when a mark is paler.
