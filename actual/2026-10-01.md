[letter tracing worksheets, A to Z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing)

shows a real worksheet page, and the settings and Download button come
straight after, with nothing running off the side. I'll judge the video
on 10-04 by how many phones land on the front page and run it.

Why bother: in the minute before the first sale of

now checks that every page's share picture loads at the size Facebook
asks for (1200 × 630), and that each worksheet page uses its own. I
broke one page on purpose to make sure the test catches it, and it did.

## Capitals alone, or lowercase alone

Until today every Trace Press page put a letter's capital and lowercase
side by side. Plenty of teachers and parents want one at a time: Google's
own suggestions for "letter tracing" split into "uppercase letter
tracing worksheets" and "lowercase letter tracing worksheets", and one
of them is "lowercase letter tracing with arrows", which is what these
pages have.

So [Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) now has a
**Letters** setting: capital and lowercase, capitals only, or lowercase
only. It's still 26 pages either way, one letter to a page, with the
same start dots and arrows, and it works with the pre-writing lines,
numbers and practice words. Two new free PDFs come from it:

- [Uppercase letter tracing worksheets, A to Z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/uppercase-letter-tracing)
- [Lowercase letter tracing worksheets, a to z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/lowercase-letter-tracing)

Both use 1" lines, the setting for ages 4–5. I looked at all 52 pages
before they went up. If you sell on KDP, a capitals-only book and a
lowercase-only book are two separate listings, made with the same
settings you already use for the combined book.

## What KDP sellers type, and a guide renamed to match

Google's suggestions show how KDP sellers phrase it: "amazon kdp tracing
book", "how to make a tracing book", "how to make a handwriting book".
The Trace Press guide was called "How to make a handwriting workbook for
Amazon KDP" and didn't say "tracing book" anywhere. It's now
[How to make a tracing book or handwriting workbook for KDP](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/how-to-make-a-handwriting-workbook).
The steps are the same: the workbook's size, line size, bleed, the
interior, the cover and the price, with printing cost and royalty for a
26-page book. The address stays the same, so no link to it breaks.
