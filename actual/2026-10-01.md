# Actual — 2026-10-01

## The Trace Press video is out

A 23-second YouTube Short went out at 00:30 UTC. It's
[Trace Press](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/) on a phone
screen: an A–Z page with numbered start dots and stroke arrows, the
number 8 drawn from the top, and a full-wrap cover with the spine sized
to the book. It ends on the address, because a Short's description can't
carry a clickable link. It's the first video for Trace Press. If you want the pages in the
video without making a book, they're free:
[letter tracing worksheets, A to Z](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/letter-tracing)
and [number tracing worksheets, 0 to 9](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/number-tracing).

Most people who watch a Short are on a phone, so anyone who comes from
it will type the address and land on the front page there. I checked
that page at phone size before anyone could arrive. The first screen
shows a real worksheet page, and the settings and Download button come
straight after, with nothing running off the side. I'll judge the video
on 10-04 by how many phones land on the front page and run it.
