# Actual — 2026-10-19

## Twelve days to Halloween: how long KDP takes

Halloween is 31 October, twelve days from today. If you're putting out a Halloween book this year, the question is whether KDP can review it and get it on sale in time. These figures are from KDP's own help page, "Timelines", read today:

- "it takes 3 business days for your book (or update) to be live on Amazon."
- For a new paperback, the detail page appears in "Up to 72 hours for Amazon.com; up to 5 days for other marketplaces."
- "for accounts without a previously published book, publishing may take up to 6 days."
- Low-content books are slower right now. KDP says "low-content book reviews are taking longer than usual right now" and may take three to four weeks. Three weeks from today is 9 November, after Halloween.

Which side of that line a book falls on matters. KDP's "Low-Content Books" help page says: "A low-content book has minimal or no content on the interior pages." It adds that they are "generally repetitive, and designed to be filled in by the user". It then says this "does not typically include activity books, such as puzzle books or coloring books."

A word search book is a puzzle book, so by KDP's own words it is not low-content. A tracing workbook is filled in by the child, and KDP doesn't name it either way. You declare it yourself with the low-content checkbox when you set the book up. Make that call before you count on a Halloween sale.

Both Halloween books here are free to make in the browser:

- a [Halloween tracing book](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/halloween-tracing-worksheets): A to Z and 20 Halloween words, from bat and ghost to pumpkin and lollipop, with the KDP cover
- [word search puzzle books](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/word-search-book-generator) with your own word lists, interior and cover together

## Seven puzzle-book tools, tested on the same day

Comparison pages are usually written from the tools' own marketing. This one is not. On 19 October we opened seven KDP puzzle-book tools in a browser. We created no account, gave no email and paid nothing, and tried to get a book out of each. Every cell on the [comparison page](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/compare) now says whether we saw it ourselves or only read it on the tool's site.

Three of the tools put a diagonal SAMPLE or brand watermark across the free puzzles. One free tool, PuzzleForge, puts no mark at all on its pages. That beats Puzzle Press's footer line, and the page says so. If you only need clean puzzle pages and will do the layout and cover yourself, use it. What none of the seven did free and without an account was hand back the whole paperback: page numbers, a gutter that follows the page count, and a full-wrap cover with the spine worked out.
