# Actual — 2026-10-16

## The KDP margin calculator takes custom trim sizes

KDP prints paperbacks at any custom size from 4" to 8.5" wide and 6" to 11.69" tall, not only the standard trims (KDP help, "Print Options"). The [KDP margin calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/margin-calculator) now has a "Custom size" choice. Type the width and height, and it gives the PDF page size, the inside, outside and top/bottom margins for your page count, and the odd and even page templates as 300 DPI PNGs.

The bleed rule is the same at every size: the PDF page is 0.125" wider and 0.25" taller. So a 7 × 10 book with bleed is a 7.125" × 10.25" PDF page. A size outside KDP's range shows the limits instead of numbers.

The Puzzle Press generator itself still makes the standard sizes in the menu. For a custom size, the calculator gives you the figures to set up your own document.

## The spine calculator takes custom sizes too

The [KDP spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) now has the same "Custom size" choice. The full cover is the back, the spine and the front side by side, plus 0.125" of bleed on every outside edge. So it's twice the trim width, plus the spine, plus 0.25" across, and the trim height plus 0.25". I checked that against KDP's own cover calculator at four custom sizes, each at 200 pages of cream paper (a 0.5" spine):

| Trim | Full cover |
|---|---|
| 4" × 6" | 8.75" × 6.25" |
| 5.25" × 7.75" | 11.25" × 8" |
| 7" × 10" | 14.75" × 10.25" |
| 8.5" × 11.69" | 17.75" × 11.94" |

The calculator gives the same figures, plus the pixel size at 300 DPI and a cover template PNG with the bleed, spine folds, safe area and barcode box drawn in.

A custom size also decides the printing price. KDP counts a trim as large when it is more than 6.12" wide or more than 9" tall, and a large trim costs more to print per copy (KDP help, "Paperback Printing Cost"). Within each class, the price depends only on the ink and the page count. So for a custom size, pick any menu trim of the same class in the [royalty calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/royalty-calculator): 6" × 9" or smaller for a regular trim, 7" × 10" or larger for a large one.
