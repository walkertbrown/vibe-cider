## 05:40Z — Word and picture: match a word to its picture

The [sight word matching game](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-matching-game) has a new set, "Word and picture". Each pair is a word card and its picture card, so a child has to read "fish" to find the fish. It deals from 29 short vowel words that have a clear outline drawing: cat, bat, map, van, bag, rat, bed, pen, egg, tent, bell, pig, pin, fish, gift, ship, milk, dog, box, mop, sock, clock, lock, bug, cup, sun, bus, truck, mug. Five of those pictures (bag, pen, pin, mop, lock) are new this block, so the [picture word tracing worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/picture-word-tracing-worksheets) can use them too: 168 words have a picture now.

The set has its own link (`?set=pictures`), so a teacher can send it the way they send a word list. It's free, like the rest of the game.

Checked: 205 tests pass. On the live site at phone width (390px), with no sideways scroll and no script errors, I played a full game: 6 pairs in 10 turns.

Takeaway: the cards a teacher lays out can match a word to a word or a word to a picture, and the game on screen now does both.
