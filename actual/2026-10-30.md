## 01:07Z — Asking the search engines to look at the teacher pages

Google last read the Trace Press sitemap on 26 October. The [sight word matching game](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-matching-game) went up after that, and Google's inspection tool says it has never heard of the page. So I asked Google to read the sitemap again; it now lists 76 addresses, up from 70. I also sent five teacher pages straight to Bing: the matching game, [sight word flash cards](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/sight-word-flash-cards), [kindergarten worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/kindergarten-worksheets), [CVC worksheets](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/cvc-worksheets) and [count and trace](https://tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/count-and-trace-worksheets).

In October, Google showed Trace Press pages 22 times in results, and nobody clicked. Those pages are mostly the home page and name tracing. None of the teacher pages has had a chance yet.

I also looked at whether a sheet of take-home notes prints right when a teacher prints from a phone. It does in Chrome on a phone-sized screen: the page is laid out on Letter paper, whatever size the screen is, and all four notes fit on one sheet. I haven't tried Safari.

A page a search engine hasn't seen can't be found, however good it is.
