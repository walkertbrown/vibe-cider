## A Short on KDP's 79-or-80 spine rule

Yesterday's finding is now a 33-second video: [KDP spine text: 79 pages or 80? KDP says both](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y8-1CpQel68). KDP's paperback cover help says "at least 79 pages" and "more than 79 pages", and Cover Creator's help says 80. A book KDP counted at 79 pages had its spine text refused, so plan on 80.

Even at 80 pages there's very little room. A 6 × 9 spine on white paper is 0.180", and after the 0.0625" KDP asks you to keep clear at each fold, 0.055" is left for text, about 3.5 pt of bold type. On cream it's 0.200" and 0.075". The [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) shows that strip for any trim, paper and page count.

Before it's drawn, the script that makes the video checks each quote word for word against KDP's live help pages, and each width against KDP's own cover calculator. If KDP changes a page, the video won't render until I've rewritten it.

## A 24-page colour picture book: the cover sizes

This week on the KDP Community, someone with a 24-page children's book thought KDP wouldn't print it, and found that Cover Creator wouldn't let them put a single picture across the back. Both have answers.

**The page count is fine for a premium colour paperback.** KDP's "Print Options" help page lists these minimums: 24 pages for a paperback in black ink or premium colour, 72 in standard colour, and 75 for any hardcover. So a 24-page picture book can be a paperback in premium colour, and not yet a hardcover.

**For the back cover, make your own.** KDP takes "a single PDF file that includes the back cover, spine, and front cover as one image", with 0.125" of bleed on the top, bottom and outside edges. Then the back can be whatever picture you like. KDP adds a barcode to the back unless you supply one, so leave its box clear (bottom right on KDP's own cover templates).

Here are the full cover sizes from KDP's cover calculator, for a premium colour paperback:

| Trim | 24 pages | 32 pages |
|---|---|---|
| 7.5" × 7.5" | 15.306" × 7.75" | 15.325" × 7.75" |
| 8.25" × 8.25" | 16.806" × 8.5" | 16.825" × 8.5" |
| 8.5" × 8.5" | 17.306" × 8.75" | 17.325" × 8.75" |
| 8" × 10" | 16.306" × 10.25" | 16.325" × 10.25" |
| 8.5" × 11" | 17.306" × 11.25" | 17.325" × 11.25" |

The spine is 0.056" at 24 pages and 0.075" at 32. That's premium colour's 0.002347" a page, and it's too thin for text: KDP's cover calculator gives a spine safe area of 0 at both counts. At 300 DPI, an 8.5 × 8.5 cover at 24 pages is 5192 × 2625 pixels.

The [spine calculator](https://puzzlepress.bananafest-destiny.com/spine-calculator) gives the same figures for any page count. Pick Premium colour, and use "Custom size" for the square trims. It also draws a 300 DPI cover template with the bleed, the spine folds and the barcode box marked. I checked all ten sizes above against KDP's calculator before writing this.
