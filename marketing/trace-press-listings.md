**Headline:** Free Short Vowel Matching Game Turns a Teacher's Hand-Cut Cards into Nine Straight Cuts

**Summary:** Trace Press, a free browser tool for printable handwriting and phonics sheets, now prints a short vowel matching game: 60 CVC words on 2.5-inch cards, twelve to a sheet, with coloured backs for double-sided printing.

**Body (about 300 words):**

A kindergarten teacher made a short vowel matching game: laminated sheets of twelve cards, each word twice. Every card then had to be cut out by hand. The cards had coloured backs, because in a matching game the cards go face down, but the backs printed on the second side didn't line up with the fronts.

Trace Press now prints that game for free. It has five sets, short a, e, i, o and u, with twelve consonant-vowel-consonant words in each, from cat, bat and hat to bug, rug and tub. Every word goes on two 2.5-inch cards, twelve cards to a US Letter or A4 sheet, so each sheet is one complete game. The cards touch, so every cut line runs straight across the sheet. Nine passes on a paper trimmer turn a sheet into twelve cards.

The coloured backs are one block of colour that runs a quarter inch past the outer cut lines. If the printer shifts the second side slightly, every card is still covered. The grid sits in the middle of the page, so the backs line up whether the printer flips on the long edge or the short edge. Each set has its own colour, so a mixed pile sorts itself after play.

All five sets are a free 20-page PDF, and the game can be made in the browser at any size with no sign-up. The same page prints Dolch sight word flash cards (the 40 pre-primer and 52 primer words) at 3x5 or 4x6.

Trace Press also makes free name tracing, letter tracing, CVC and count-and-trace worksheets. For $19, paid once, teachers can put their own word lists on the cards and sheets.
