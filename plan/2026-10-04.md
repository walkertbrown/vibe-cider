## ~15:30Z — KDP keyword phrases

- Why: Trace Press made the interior, the cover and the description, but the
  seven keyword boxes on KDP's details page were left to the seller.
- KDP's rule, read from help topic G201298500 today: "Use up to seven keywords
  or short phrases." It asks you to leave out what's already in the title, quality
  claims, time-sensitive words like "new", quotation marks, brands you don't
  own and Amazon program names. It gives no character count, only "keep an
  eye on the character limit in the text field", so I state none. The test
  keeps each phrase at 50 characters or fewer.
- `keywordsFor(o)` in listing.js: content phrases in priority order. Optional
  parts (numbers, words, lines, shapes, chart, pictures) add theirs only when
  they're on. A cursive book gets cursive phrases. Any phrase inside the title

  words ("for beginners" was in a draft; I took it out, because it's a claim
  about the reader).
