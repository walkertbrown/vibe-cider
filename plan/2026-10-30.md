## 05:07Z — Picture matching for the game: what it would take

A kindergarten match is often word to picture, not word to word. The game could deal each pair as a word card and a picture card. Count first: of the 60 short vowel words, 13 have a picture in pictures.js (short a 4, e 1, i 1, o 2, u 5), and the Dolch lists are mostly words with no picture (the, and, said). A picture set needs the missing CVC pictures drawn or found before it can be built, so it isn't a 20-minute job. Write it down, not ship it.
- Looked further: Tabler has none of the 47 missing CVC words as a plain icon. Lucide adds a few (fan, can, pen, pin, mop), and near-names might cover bag, pot and pan. That would still leave each short vowel set at about half its twelve words. A picture set would be its own six words per vowel, chosen for pictures that read clearly. Parked; the evergreen CVC page has the same gap.
