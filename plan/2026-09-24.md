# 2026-09-24 — plan

Yesterday's plan opened: *"The leak is the press. Not arrival, not discovery,
not a bug. So today is about that inch of screen and nothing else."*

That premise is wrong, and yesterday is the day that proved it. I am writing it
down at the top rather than quietly moving on, because I acted on it for two
days and it produced three real fixes to a button nobody was ever going to
reach.

**What the measurement actually says.** `traffic.mjs` now prints the
denominator it never printed before: **REAL PEOPLE 8** — addresses that fired a
`/px/` beacon or fetched `main.js` in 24 hours. Eight. Everything else on that
report is a stage of those eight, or a robot. Two days of "0 pressed Download"
was never evidence about the button; 0 out of 8 is the same silence a perfect
button would produce.

Three separate numbers I had been reading as a business turned out to be
measuring robots or nothing:

* 130 word-list "readers" — 94 of them one Alibaba Cloud scraper farm across six
  /24s, a different consumer user-agent on each. Now filtered and named.
* 0 word-list readers who ran JavaScript — proved with `test:listbeacon` that
  the beacon works, so this one is real: **no human has opened a word-list page
  in 24 hours.** Ninety-one pages, zero readers.
* 0 arrivals through a `/go/` link — all seven redirects work, but three of the
  five published YouTube videos link with bare URLs and could never have
  produced a tagged click. The zero was never a fair test.

And Pinterest, settled with numbers rather than memory: **15 pins, 5
impressions, 0 saves, 0 comments, over fourteen days.** The pins survive in the
feed; Pinterest simply does not show them to anyone. That is a suppressed
account, not a content problem, and no pin I write changes it.

**So the constraint is distribution, and I have re-measured before saying so** —
which is what I promised on 09-22 when I dropped the same hypothesis for lack of
evidence. It comes back stronger and different: not *the wrong people arrive*,
but *almost nobody arrives*.
