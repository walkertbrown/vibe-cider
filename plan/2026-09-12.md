# Plan — 2026-09-12

## Phase 35 plan — KDP margin calculator

The third small tool. "KDP margins" / "KDP gutter" / "KDP page size with
bleed" are searched by every first-time publisher at the moment they are
setting up a document, and the answer depends on three inputs (trim, page
count, bleed) that people get wrong in combination. The spine calculator
has shown the pattern works; this one reuses `pageGeometry` and
`gutterInches` from the PDF engine, so the page and the product cannot
disagree.

**Build:** `/margin-calculator` — trim, page count, bleed on/off → PDF page
size (inches, mm, points), inside/outside/top/bottom margins, which side the
gutter is on for odd and even pages, a drawn diagram of a left-hand and a
right-hand page with the safe area shaded, and a "Word / InDesign settings"
line (mirror margins, inside = gutter). The gutter table, the bleed rule,
and the two mistakes people make (gutter on the wrong side; forgetting the
page grows with bleed). Links to the spine and royalty calculators and the
guide. Sitemap, footer links, live test.

Needs from boss: nothing.

## Phase 39 plan — a fourth type: criss-cross (word fill-in) puzzles

Every launch reply will ask "crosswords?". A real crossword needs clues and
a fill I cannot make good in two days. A **criss-cross / fill-in** — the
grid of a crossword with the word list printed instead of clues, solved by
fitting words by length and crossing letters — needs no clues, is a
recognised KDP category ("word fill-in books"), and reuses the themed lists.

**Build:** `src/generator/crisscross.js`: place words from a list into a
crossword-style grid (first word across the middle, then each next word
crossing an existing one at a shared letter, longest first, backtracking on
failure), reject grids where a placement creates an unintended adjacent
word, verify **exactly one solution** by solving it (fit words by length
into slots with backtracking; count solutions with a cap of 2, like the
sudoku). Grade by word count and grid size. Render: numbered black-and-
white grid, word list grouped by length beneath; solution as the filled
grid. Wire into the tool as a fourth `kind`, into the cover, the preview,
the samples and the type pages. Unit tests: every word placed exactly
once, every slot filled, unique solution, deterministic by seed.

**Ships only if** all tests pass, a 50-puzzle book renders clean through
Ghostscript, and the cold read of the page holds — by Monday noon. If not,
it stays off the launch and the three working types launch alone.

Needs from boss: nothing.
