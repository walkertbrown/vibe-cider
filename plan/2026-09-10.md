# Plan — 2026-09-10

## Decision: Puzzle Press

A web app that generates a complete, print-ready word-search puzzle book as a PDF, to Amazon KDP specifications, entirely in the browser.

**Why this one**

- Buyers already pay for this. KDP "low-content" publishers buy puzzle-generation tools today (subscriptions around $10/month, or desktop tools at $30–100). A pay-once web tool at a lower price is a clear offer.
- Free alternatives make single puzzles, not books. The value is the batch: 50–100 unique puzzles, themed, with solution pages, at the right trim size with the right margins and bleed, in one PDF that KDP accepts. That is what people currently pay for.
- No backend. Generation and PDF rendering run client-side. Deploys as static assets on a Cloudflare Worker. No database, no accounts, no email. Nothing I am forbidden from opening is needed.
- Payment fits the rules. Free tier: up to 5 puzzles per PDF, with a footer watermark. Paid: unlimited, no watermark. The unlock is a pay link the boss gives me plus a license check. I write no key-minting code.
- Extensible without starting a new app. Sudoku, mazes, crosswords, and cryptograms are the same product (same buyers, same PDF pipeline). Updates, not a second app.
- Findable audience. r/KDP, r/selfpublish, KDP Facebook groups, YouTube "low content book" channels. Search intent exists ("word search book generator", "KDP puzzle generator").

**Not chosen (and why)**: screenshot beautifier (strong free competitors, low conversion), league scheduler (real pain but slow discovery), wedding seating chart (free incumbents win).

## Phase 2 plan — PDF pipeline (added after phase 1 shipped)

Goal: `renderBook(book, settings) -> PDF bytes` that Amazon KDP accepts as a manuscript without warnings.

- `app/src/pdf/kdp.js` — trim sizes (6×9, 8.5×11, 8×10, 7×10, 5.5×8.5, 5×8), KDP margin table (gutter by page count: ≤150 → 0.375", ≤300 → 0.5", ≤500 → 0.625", ≤700 → 0.75"), bleed on/off (bleed adds 0.125" on outside edges, outer margin ≥ 0.375"), minimum 24 pages, even page count.
- `app/src/pdf/render.js` — pdf-lib. Pages: title page, optional copyright page, one puzzle per page (grid + word bank in columns), solutions at 4 per page with placed cells shaded, page numbers on the outside corner, blank "Notes" pages to reach 24 pages if needed.
- Fonts embedded and subset (Liberation Sans, SIL OFL — KDP flags un-embedded fonts). Files in `app/public/fonts/`.
- Free-tier watermark: footer line "Made with Puzzle Press — free preview" on every page; caller passes `licensed: true` to omit it. The 5-puzzle limit is enforced in the UI layer, not here.
- Tests: page count and size; margins respected (no drawing inside the gutter); a rendered 6×9 and 8.5×11 sample written to `app/samples/` and opened by me to check.
- Tooling: esbuild to bundle for the browser (phase 3 uses it). No framework.

Needs from boss: nothing.

## Phase 3 plan — UI + free tier (added after phase 2 shipped)

Goal: a stranger opens one page, makes a book, downloads the PDF. No account.

- `app/public/index.html` + `app/src/ui/main.js`, bundled with esbuild to `app/public/app.js`. Plain HTML/CSS/JS. No framework. Works on a phone width.
- Left: settings. Book title, subtitle, author. Word source: pick built-in themes (multi) and/or paste your own list (one per line, or comma-separated; a "Theme name" field for it). Puzzle count (5–100), words per puzzle (8–25), difficulty, trim size, bleed on/off, seed (auto, with a "reshuffle" button).
- Right: live preview of puzzle 1 drawn as HTML (so it's instant), warnings from the generator (dropped words, didn't fit), page-count estimate and KDP print-cost hint is *not* included (I don't have KDP's price table verified).
- "Download PDF": generates in the browser. Fonts fetched from `/fonts/`. Free tier: if not licensed, cap at 5 puzzles and keep the watermark. Show the cap plainly next to the button with the price and a "Buy" link placeholder that is disabled until the boss gives a pay URL (phase 5).
- License state: `localStorage` flag set in phase 5; UI reads it now so phase 5 is only the verify route.
- A "Sample book" link to `samples/sample-6x9.pdf` so a visitor can see the output before doing anything.
- Test: `npm run build` produces `public/app.js`; open it with a local static server; make a 5-puzzle and a 60-puzzle (licensed, via a dev flag) book; open both PDFs.

Needs from boss: nothing.

## Phase 4 plan — deploy (added after phase 3 shipped)

Goal: live at `puzzle-press.<account>.workers.dev`, opened by me, both downloads working there.

- Deploy with `wrangler deploy` using `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` from the environment, never written to the repo.
- Test: run `test/browser.mjs` against the live URL, not just localhost.

## Phase 6 plan — marketing (added after phase 5 shipped)

The constraint the boss gave me: Reddit account is new and puzzle/KDP subs don't allow advertising. So I cannot buy attention and I cannot spam. What I can do is make the page itself do the selling and produce artifacts other people can pass around.

Three levers, in order of what I control:

**1. The page must convert a stranger.** Today it opens as a tool with a one-line header — no reason to trust it, no price visible until you open a dialog. Rewrite as a landing page that still has the tool above the fold:
- Hero: what it makes, who for, proof link to the sample book.
- "Why your book won't be rejected" — the KDP spec work (trim, gutter by page count, bleed, embedded fonts, 24-page minimum) stated as buyer benefits.
- "Why the puzzles are correct" — every word once, no nested words, screened filler. This is the real differentiator over free single-puzzle generators.
- Pricing block with the Buy button visible on the page, not only behind the unlock dialog.
- FAQ answering what KDP beginners actually search: margins for a given page count, which trim sizes, bleed or no bleed, can I sell what I make (yes — I claim no rights).

**2. Be findable.** The FAQ above is the SEO play — real answers to real searches, on a page that happens to hold the tool. Plus `<title>`/description written for "KDP word search book generator", Open Graph + Twitter card so shared links preview properly, JSON-LD (SoftwareApplication + FAQPage), `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`.

**3. Artifacts that travel.** A 1200×630 social card and a short demo the boss can post anywhere — rendered from the real app with Playwright, not mocked up.

Tests: rebuild, redeploy, re-run the Playwright e2e against live, check the OG image and structured data actually parse.

Needs from boss: nothing to build it. To distribute it, the asks are at the end of this file's actual entry.

## Phase 7 plan — pre-launch value (added after phase 6 shipped)

The app is sellable and nobody has bought it. Everything here is chosen for one
test: does it make a KDP seller more likely to pay on Tuesday?

1. **Gallery slots 4 and 5** — the tool with its preview showing, and a solutions
   page close-up. Promised for the launch.
2. **Themes, 10 → 30+.** The single cheapest increase in perceived value. Ten
   themes is thin for someone planning a series of books, and the ones I shipped
   are generic. KDP sellers publish *seasonal and giftable*: Halloween,
   Thanksgiving, Easter, Valentine's, birthdays, weddings, US states, farm,
   dinosaurs, camping, fishing, cars, weather, body/health, jobs, colors,
   school, baby, tea/coffee, wine, knitting, birds, bugs, trees, flowers, fruit,
   desserts, breakfast, movie night, board games. A seller picking a niche needs
   the niche to exist in the picker.
3. **Large print preset.** The biggest sub-niche in puzzle books is large-print
   for older solvers. It is already possible (8.5×11, fewer words) but nobody
   discovers it. One checkbox that sets trim, grid size and word count, and says
   "Large print" on the page, turns a hidden capability into a reason to buy.
4. **Re-run the full purchase test against live on Monday**, so nothing has
   drifted before the launch window.

Not doing yet, on purpose: sudoku and mazes (post-launch updates, same pipeline),
and a cover generator. A cover is the real gap between "interior PDF" and
"published book" — spine width from page count and paper stock, wrap and bleed —
and it is the strongest candidate for the next update, but the specs are exacting
and shipping it wrong is worse than not shipping it. Decide after launch, on what
people actually ask for.

Needs from boss: nothing.
