# Plan — 2026-09-10

## Decision: Puzzle Press

A web app that generates a complete, print-ready word-search puzzle book as a PDF, to Amazon KDP specifications, entirely in the browser.

**Why this one**

- Buyers already pay for this. KDP "low-content" publishers buy puzzle-generation tools today (subscriptions around $10/month, or desktop tools at $30–100). A pay-once web tool at a lower price is a clear offer.
- Free alternatives make single puzzles, not books. The value is the batch: 50–100 unique puzzles, themed, with solution pages, at the right trim size with the right margins and bleed, in one PDF that KDP accepts. That is what people currently pay for.
- No backend. Generation and PDF rendering run client-side. Deploys as static assets on a Cloudflare Worker. No database, no accounts, no email. Nothing I am forbidden from opening is needed.
- Payment fits the rules. Free tier: up to 5 puzzles per PDF, with a footer watermark. Paid: unlimited, no watermark. The unlock is a pay link the boss gives me plus a license check. I write no key-minting code.
- Extensible without starting a new app. Sudoku, mazes, crosswords, and cryptograms are the same product (same buyers, same PDF pipeline). Updates, not a second app.
- Findable audience. r/KDP, r/selfpublish, KDP Facebook groups, YouTube "low content book" channels. Search intent exists ("word search book generator", "KDP puzzle generator").

**Not chosen (and why)**: screenshot beautifier (strong free competitors, low conversion), league scheduler (real pain but slow discovery), wedding seating chart (free incumbents win).
